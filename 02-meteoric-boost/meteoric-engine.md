# The Meteoric Engine and the delivery system

How Meteoric Boost delivers a founder's personal brand: the published 5-stage, 12-week Meteoric Engine from the site; the process as Moksh tells it step by step in July 2026 (extraction call of 41 to 50 questions, internal brainstorm, production, data review); and the delivery methods he and his clients describe elsewhere (extracting the edge, attractive character, trademark positioning, profile, aesthetics, formats, scripting, editors, story sequences, analytics, sprints, onboarding, community and retention).

Context for a cold reader: Meteoric Boost is Moksh Vasant's personal brand studio for founders earning $50K/month or more. The 2026 offer is a 3-month done-for-you partnership where the founder spends 4 to 8 hours a month on camera and the studio runs the rest. In 2025 to mid 2026 the same delivery was sold and described as done-with-you (see [company.md](company.md) section 2). Results figures are self-reported.

## 1. The Engine as published (site, Oct 2026)

Headline figures: **14 phases, 5 stages, 12 weeks, 4 owners.** The site says two phases are internal and never shown. Only the five stages are public. The 14 phase names were not found anywhere.

| # | Stage | Weeks | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Extract | 1 to 2 | Pull out everything true about the founder before building anything |
| 02 | Build Your World | 2 to 4 | Positioning, formats, visuals, wardrobe, and the specific audience |
| 03 | Show Up | 3 to 12 | The founder's only job: arrive dressed as directed, at the chosen location, with scripts ready |
| 04 | Production | 3 to 12 | Filmed, cut, quality-checked, posted |
| 05 | Compound | 6 to 12 | Weekly review, sharpen, repeat |

- Stages overlap on purpose. Content goes live from about week 3 while positioning is still being finished. Review loops start in week 6.
- FAQ version: onboarding, a brand strategy session, the content system build, then a loop of recording and posting with weekly reviews.
- Results timing per the FAQ: content live in the first weeks; distribution and inbound build across the three months.

### Deliverables listed on the site

| Component | What's included |
|---|---|
| Brand Positioning | 1 locked roadmap |
| Content Strategy | 3 formats per founder |
| Founder Positioning | 1 one-of-one angle |
| Creative Direction | 1 deep session |
| Distribution | 7 sequence types |
| Content Production | Weekly, in batches |
| Editing | Every asset reviewed |
| Analytics and Iteration | Reviewed weekly |

### Operating rules (site)
1. If the team can do a task, the founder never touches it.
2. If a document can be built from a transcript, it is never a form.
3. If a decision can be made internally, the founder does not sit in a meeting for it.
4. Every phase has a named owner and a fixed week.

### Principles (site, "Why Meteoric")
| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| It's not homework | The studio builds and runs the engine. No education, worksheets or deliverables handed back to the founder |
| Extraction, not invention | A brand built as a costume shows. Work starts from what is already true about the founder |
| The room you're never in | Three principals work on the brand without the founder on the call. The founder gets the conclusion, not the meeting |
| 4 to 8 hours a month | Camera time is the whole ask. If it takes more, the system was built wrong |
| Taste is the deliverable | The standard of work is visible before anyone pays |
| One of one | No templates. Each founder gets a single angle nobody else holds |

Moksh says he personally runs the creative vision layer on every client brand (site founder note).

## 4. Stage 1, Extract

### Extract Your Edge (Aug to Sep 2025 detail)
1. **Ambitions.** 5 to 10 year goals for the person and the brand (impact, money, travel, creative fulfilment). "A personal brand is definitely a game that I want to play infinitely, at least over the next decade" (qYIKwqRvIyU [05:30]).
2. **Ideal Viewer Profile (IVP).** Who watches. His own: ambitious, young, smart Indians who relate to his life. Learned from Saul "over the past 3 months" (as of Sep 2025, qYIKwqRvIyU [06:46]).
3. **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).** Who buys. His own: founders and wealth creators.
4. **Ideal Investor Profile.** Added Aug 2025: design the brand to attract capital too (H68gyWOlIy4).
5. **Attractive character.** Russell Brunson's archetypes (leader, adventurer, reporter, reluctant hero). Pick two and hold them about 18 months. Moksh's pair: adventurer (reach) and leader (authority and sales). Jan 2026 list: leader, adventurer, entertainer, reluctant hero (BU0smWywaw8 [09:22]).
6. **Backstory and parables.** An unfiltered 1,500 to 2,000 word autobiography (a transcribed voice note works); 5 or 6 parables woven into scripts.
7. **Unfair advantages.** Named without apology: accent, looks, fitness, intelligence, taste, an upbringing that allowed failure.
8. **Inspiration from outside the niche.** "I tell them not to mention any creator" (qYIKwqRvIyU [16:30]); use music, films, fiction (James Bond, Harvey Specter). "You want to be a purple cow in a herd of black and white cows" (H68gyWOlIy4 [19:25]).

### The attractive character, four parts (May 2026)
"Most founders build content around their offer. The strongest brands build content around their character and the offer becomes a natural extension" (qSNYWCyKGyo [24:23]). "It's not performance. It's the most amplified, most deliberate version of what's already true about you" [24:37].
1. **Backstory:** "story creates empathy before authority"; a backstory video first for every client [25:26].
2. **Strong opinions:** "vanilla brands don't convert". Test: "would you say that if the camera was off? Yes, then go ahead and say it on camera" [25:40].
3. **Aspirational reality:** "your life documented not performed" [26:09].
4. **Nemesis:** a status quo to fight (Arjun fights comfort and inaction) [26:39 to 27:10].

### India note
India "is a trust-deficient economy, so having a niche personal brand does not always yield the highest outcomes"; with 100K+ followers "people just put you on a pedestal", which helps on sales calls (qYIKwqRvIyU [07:14 to 07:27]). An earlier version of this file said "trust-driven"; the transcript says "trust-deficient".

### Intake documents
- A long onboarding form that makes the client reflect and productises their expertise; Moksh reads it back to them on camera (sD4R8Z4H6U4 [00:12], [21:38]).
- A "diary" document: "treat it like your diary and write everything about yourself in it" (1h29rLdXY4I [09:29 to 09:42]).
- Journaling prompts: how he wants to present himself, the future brand, who he talks to, what makes him special, who he admires (uncAf4Iuqdk [06:52 to 07:49]).

### Qualification on the first call
Moksh judges whether a profile is "easy to scale": real authority, revenue and an interesting life, lacking only distribution and status (sD4R8Z4H6U4). Heavy hitters' problem is "transferring that authority from real life into their social media profile" (C4ckCV1xmj8 [06:02]).

## 5. Stage 2, Build Your World

### Brand formula
- About a two-hour call, from inspirations the client sends (Trisha: Sabyasachi, Western rug sellers) (1h29rLdXY4I [08:56 to 09:17]); "the one-on-one brand formula" (KrPQX-qdYRI [04:51]).
- **Life buckets.** 3 or 4 life pursuits; business is always one. Moksh's: personal branding, online business, lifestyle.
- **Formats.** Each with one job (section 7). Client default: three formats, informative, raw connection, reach (qYIKwqRvIyU [31:11]). "If you're trying to get reach while trying to get leads, you're not going to solve either problems" [30:47].
- **Visual identity** set by the creative director, who gets on a call to learn the client's taste (qSNYWCyKGyo [20:29 to 21:15]).
- **Funnel and cadence.**

### Trademark, not niche (May 2026)
"A niche is a category. Hundreds of people operate in your niche. A trademark is a universe. Only you occupy it." "Familiar does not convert" (qSNYWCyKGyo [20:16 to 20:29]). Arjun closing deals shirtless from beaches is "a trademark. That is not a niche" [18:57 to 19:37].

**Client universes** [22:31 to 24:23]; the test is "none of these look like each other":

| Client | Universe |
|---|---|
| Dev Shah | "Unfiltered financial authority, Wolf of Wall Street energy" |
| Aryan Mahajan | "AI meets Miami or AI meets LA" |
| Deepak | "The monk founder" |
| Karan Thacker | "Goldman by day and then DJ by night" |

Story stacking does the same work: a DJ posting sets becomes "a VP at Goldman Sachs who DJs", "that level of trust is just compounded" (k57qG2-WnwI [08:32 to 08:57]).

### Positioning as language (Jul 2026)
"Squirrels talk to squirrels in a certain manner"; each echelon of wealth or industry has its own jargon. Meteoric learned the Fortune 500 operators' language and "baked it into exactly what he said in front of the camera" for Aryan Mahajan; learned how collectors value art for Trisha (w5ONkdV9OZw [05:38 to 07:20]). "Speak to them as if they are your peers" [06:47]. "We help you build a room with the exact people you want sitting inside it" [08:18]. In Apr 2026: "every single word that you say really matters" (AnqK0H6Uhis [15:29]).

### Persona and authority transfer
- Reference profiles plus fictional or film characters pin down the persona (sD4R8Z4H6U4).
- Find where credibility already lives (for Aryan, LinkedIn, about 95% of his business) and carry it to Instagram with a different flavour; don't cross-post (sD4R8Z4H6U4).
- "You shape your own narrative and that's like the best asset you could have" (Moksh, sD4R8Z4H6U4 [19:14]). The "propaganda" framing in that video is the client's.

### Goal-matched builds
No single playbook. Some clients want reach; others a narrow following of the right people; some use content only for trust before outbound (Ze4JOAAvAq8; n1vbE3e8XuE [45:38]). "Stop worrying about the followers and the views ... is my ideal client going to look at this and go, 'Yes, he knows his shit'" (oQeuKTBKVIY [06:19]). Coaches: "can they actually be the living embodiment"; "the documentation of that pursuit is what builds your brand" (z_dxsfu9NZY [03:21], [13:30]).

### Substance checklist (Sep 2026)
Content fails for lack of substance, not format (Ze4JOAAvAq8): the range ("9:00 a.m. conviction to the 7:00 p.m. feeling of doubt", [02:14 to 03:36]); current moves and fears; authority with taste; wins and momentum; audience quality; contrarian takes.

### High-status aesthetics (Mar 2026, HPCsujvgIvE)
Scripts and tools are commodities; taste is the moat. A brand is "a sort of a world that people can be a part of rather than just a piece of content that they watch" [01:07].
- Principles: less is more; effort that looks effortless; show life in motion; "Your life has to look like a movie, feel like a movie through and through" [10:04]; flex taste, not money ("The actual top 1% does not care about you showing them that you're making a lot of money, but they care about taste" [11:32]); post for one specific person [16:42]; overshare slightly ("if it backfires, you can always delete it" [15:44]).
- Mood board of the life you want, built before you can afford it, not a "manifestation" board [19:03 to 19:17].
- **Starter pack:** a few repeated signifiers: his Omega Seamaster, linen shirts, a black bag, a scent [26:48 to 27:36]; "linen pants, linen shirts, that's all I wear" (qSNYWCyKGyo [30:45]). Clients' packs: Manav's hat, beard and orange Whoop; Arjun's coconuts and beaches (qSNYWCyKGyo [30:28 to 31:18]). They "occupy mental real estate".
- **Shot angles as psychology:** side = "in my element"; front gaze = authority; back = "follow the leader psychology"; training clips = "pursuit of a superior self"; an occasional fun selfie [22:23 to 23:17]. A **sunlight hook reserve**: a stock of sunlit-face shots because "I know that hook is going to work" [24:03].
- **Colour grade, five palettes** (not four): deep charcoal blacks with warm undertones; industrial and urban; warm desert and sunset; natural and tropical; universal blue. Intense videos use industrial plus charcoal plus warm desert; laid-back ones tropical plus blue [30:58 to 32:56]. Target: "old money elegance plus young rich energy" [28:47]. Test: "if they notice the grading, we have failed as a creative team" [30:11]. (He says avoid "orange or teal color wheels" while his industrial palette uses teal shadows and orange highlights.)
- **B-roll categories** (n1vbE3e8XuE [25:53 to 29:02]): work (desk from back, front or top, side; calls), lifestyle in motion (driving, dinner, walking, tying shoelaces), objects (shoes, steering wheel, tyre), POV, fitness. Seasonal grades: warmer and darker in winter, ocean blues in summer [28:06 to 28:42]. Tailor to what the client has ("take your Porsche").

## 6. Stage 3, Show Up

- The founder arrives dressed as directed, at the chosen location, with scripts ready (site); "We tell you exactly where to film, exactly what to say, exactly what to wear" (w5ONkdV9OZw [09:44]).
- **Filming support ladder:** remote camera setup over FaceTime, then a local crew (sD4R8Z4H6U4); videographers sourced on demand in any city, "within 24 hours" (w5ONkdV9OZw [10:30]); the creative director found one in Kochi "in 1 minute" (1h29rLdXY4I [12:20 to 12:41]).
- **Visual world on location:** the office and filmable spots around it, "Can they film in the car?", "where in their factory can we place the camera?", outfits that fit their style (pyYGdcb1V48 [05:20 to 05:45]). Abundant clients allow bigger plays: "get them to get a mansion for a shoot" [02:23].
- **Speed:** "lock a shoot for the weekend, book the videographer and location, film Saturday, send feedback"; "the people that move the quickest and are able to give us feedback in the process are the people that scale quick" (pyYGdcb1V48 [05:45 to 06:19]).
- **Voiceover-only start** on request (Trisha, first four weeks, 1h29rLdXY4I [13:49]).
- **Batching (his own brand, Sep 2025):** about 2 days a month (filming; scripting and voiceovers) for 6 to 8 videos (qYIKwqRvIyU).
- **Hook prep on set** (n1vbE3e8XuE [24:22 to 25:38]): good sunlight, a good outfit with the face visible, the unfair advantage on show, a polarising line. "What you say needs to have credibility and authority and a bit of flexing, what you show needs to just like interrupt someone's pattern."

## 7. Stage 4, Production

### Scripting
- Moksh writes his own scripts by hand (n1vbE3e8XuE [29:40]).
- For clients, an internal Claude workflow: context from the first couple of weeks plus a custom prompt; each client's voice matched. "It should not sound like AI. That's the whole point ... we nailed our AI assistant to the point where it never sounds like AI" (n1vbE3e8XuE [29:54 to 30:52]). "We'll set up Claude projects for every single format" (AnqK0H6Uhis [1:21:02]). Claude prompts and a Google Drive per client (KrPQX-qdYRI [05:03]).
- Hook types: a specific scene ("4:00 a.m. Paramount Hotel balcony. I was chain smoking cigarettes with two millionaires from private equity") or "the classic... as a 22-year-old or a 24-year-old" (n1vbE3e8XuE [30:19]).
- Moksh sits in on the first scripts: Kevil had him for the first five or six (oBxRdpeTjaY [04:54 to 05:06]); Adi handles reviews and scripting for some clients (oQeuKTBKVIY [07:47]).
- First client video: "It's so origin story based ... has to be a banger, right? It has to be a win" (vT7pvIRtxaM [13:47 to 14:01]); backstory video first (qSNYWCyKGyo [25:26]).

### Reel formula (TSS, Mar 2026)
The widest hook ("What's the widest total addressable market hook I can use?" [16:25]; "as a 20-year-old" fits anyone), a pattern-interrupt opening visual, a cut every 1 to 1.5 seconds, house music at 120 to 140 BPM, a flowing script, about 60 seconds, posted about 30 minutes before peak. "If you're able to nail the hook in the first four to five clips, you have a video that will perform well" [31:01]. Prove a format twice, write the SOP, hand it to an editor [18:34 to 19:00].

### Engagement rate (Sep 2025, from his own data)
Engagement rate = (likes + saves + shares + DMs + comments + follows from that viewer) divided by views, times 100 (qYIKwqRvIyU).

| ER | What he says happens |
|---|---|
| Above 7.5% | "Reaches a fairly wide audience" |
| Above 9% | About 60% of viewers are new |
| Above 11% | About 90% of viewers are new |
| Above 14% | Viral |

That's like the three main things of value", with motivation, aspiration, information and entertainment also named [45:16 to 45:40]. (Earlier versions said "four value types".)

## 11. Onboarding run sheet (derived for agents)

A synthesis of the sources above, not a published Meteoric document. Confirm details with Moksh or the COO.

1. **Before kickoff:** read the application, fit-call notes and recording. Run the 3-second profile test.
2. **Day 0:** same-day onboarding by Moksh; automated contract, payment, forms and portal; a roadmap within 24 hours; client added to the community.
3. **Week 1 to 2, Extract:** onboarding form and backstory; the 41 to 50 question team interview, recorded; draft documents from the transcript: ambitions (life and brand), IVP (including "their average Tuesday"), ICP, archetype and attractive character (backstory, strong opinions, aspirational reality, nemesis), parables, unfair advantages, out-of-niche inspirations, where existing authority lives, visual worlds (car, outfits, music, hangouts).
4. **Next day, internal brainstorm (no client):** brand formula, one-of-one angle (trademark), editor team, formats (2 or 3, each with a job), frequency, execution needs; videographer and scripting engine.
5. **Week 2 to 4, Build Your World:** creative direction call; starter pack, shot list, grade; profile rebuild (picture, bio, link, highlights, pins); story-sequence plan; offer review if there is a back end; client picks the editor.
6. **From week 3, Show Up and Production:** backstory video first; scripts from the Claude workflow in the client's voice; weekend shoots with location and wardrobe set; team QC; first quick win targeted in month 1.
7. **From week 6, Compound:** weekly review (ER against thresholds, audience-quality audit, sequence metrics); Moksh's data pass; double down and strip the rest; bulk-or-cut call; sprint or pause; hire a setter if leads are flowing.
8. **At results:** fly in to film the testimonial with the 11-question structure; clip it for the perception stack.
