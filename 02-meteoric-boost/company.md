# Meteoric Boost: company profile

Meteoric Boost (often just "Meteoric") is Moksh Vasant's personal brand studio for founders. It was conceived in Sydney in April 2025, signed its first clients in June 2025, and runs from India. This file covers what it is, where the name came from, how the model changed, who is on the team and where they are, tools, revenue claims over time, funnel and paid-media changes, the events company and the Bali retreat.

All revenue, client and team numbers are self-reported by Moksh, his team, clients or the company site. None are independently verified. Where sources disagree, both figures are kept. Facts tagged **[Aarya, 20 Oct 2026]** come privately from Aarya (Fulcrum Labs), not from Moksh's public content.

## 1. Name history: from calisthenics to studio

- **Meteoric v1, May 2021 to about early 2022 (contemporaneous videos; it ended when both founders left for university, and Moksh started in Sydney in Feb 2022).** At 17, at the start of the gap after his CBSE class 12 boards were postponed (and later cancelled), Moksh and a friend (Whisper "Dhannil") built an online calisthenics coaching startup called Meteoric. "We will be entrepreneurs on the 23rd of May" (yfVG-XkTcBw [02:35], May 2021); prototype on two friends late May to early June; announced 15 Jun 2021 with the Instagram @meteoricboost (pXLF_xVQdPA [01:26]). Still active in Aug, Oct and Dec 2021 (bGt5b2CIelI [04:29]).
- **Later retellings date it to 2020.** "A calisthenics coaching startup in like in 2020 called Meteoric" (6SYJFqH-RPQ [10:17]); "2020", from a 12th-grade tuition-class product launch that started with phone covers (9Ma2bozEqGM [1:05:06 to 1:05:21]); "back in I don't know 2020 2021" (n1vbE3e8XuE [06:25]). The 2021 videos are contemporaneous and match his age (born 16 Dec 2003), so prefer May to Jun 2021 unless Moksh says 2020 was an earlier idea.
- **Name origin.** A best friend's line when Moksh was low, "it's time to go from mediocre to meteoric", plus "I wanted to be an astronaut in the third grade" (yfVG-XkTcBw [01:40 to 01:59]). Clients were called "astronauts" (pXLF_xVQdPA [05:38]).
- **What v1 sold.** Workout plans matched to level and equipment ("the only prerequisite to perform our workouts is gravity"), calorie tracking, a per-client tracker, a weekly leaderboard (pXLF_xVQdPA [03:21 to 06:31]); Notion templates for tracking, "but we weren't selling templates" (Y0fC8dizX0o [28:08]).
- **v1 numbers (self-reported, told in 2024 to 2026).** "Trained 15 clients in three months" (G0UVN3E2ZKU [00:00]); up to 15 at once (Epiphanies #2); 500 rupees for 2 weeks or 1,000 rupees for 5; "some what 35 40 clients"; the first $1,000 "back in the 12th grade"; "40 45,000 rupees each, which is like 500 bucks each" over four or five months, kept in a drawer (9Ma2bozEqGM [1:07:04 to 1:08:28]). The co-founders "had always been unfit kids" (pXLF_xVQdPA [02:58]). Its stated motto was learning, not money (yfVG-XkTcBw [02:35]).
- **Why it died.** It ran until "fulfillment broke" when both went to university (9Ma2bozEqGM [1:09:26]); messy systems and burnout (Epiphanies #2): "Good systems are the backbone of scaling anything." The Engine's named owners and fixed weeks read as an answer to that failure (inference).
- **The co-founder now.** In Aug 2026 Moksh says his v1 partner runs a fitness coaching offer whose brand Meteoric built (9Ma2bozEqGM [1:06:01 to 1:06:14]). That is very probably Dhanil Shah (see [case-studies.md](case-studies.md) 3.7). Inference.
- **April 2025, the revival.** After his PE boss's farewell talk on a Tuesday and his final lecture on a Wednesday, he walked at night ("across the Darlington road behind ABBA", as captioned), tried "Nirvana Studios" and "Blank Studios" on GoDaddy, found them generic, and went back to Meteoric because "I have an emotional connection to it" (6SYJFqH-RPQ [10:06 to 11:08]). In Oct 2025 he told it as a late-night "I want to start a creative agency" idea, a walk and an Apple Notes plan (target clients, income goals, team needs), followed by paying a mentor "to sort of help me ... fast track this journey" (IEuERGHU3wA [07:01 to 07:26]).
