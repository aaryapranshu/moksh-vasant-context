# Meteoric Boost: company profile

Meteoric Boost (often just "Meteoric") is Moksh Vasant's personal brand studio for founders, started in 2025 in Sydney and run from India; this file covers what it is, where the name came from, how it grew, who is on the team, where it operates, what it has claimed in revenue, and where it lives online.

All revenue, client and team numbers below are self-reported by Moksh or by the company's own site. None have been independently verified. Where sources disagree, both figures are kept.

## Name history: from calisthenics to studio

- **2020, Meteoric v1.** Moksh co-founded an online calisthenics coaching startup called Meteoric with his best friend from high school. He gives his age then as 17. Since he turned 21 in December 2024, he would have been 16 for most of 2020, so either the year or the age is slightly off (one podcast caption places it around 2021, and a vlog places a startup co-founded with a friend in his Aug 2021 to Feb 2022 gap year, yqZDRElvh-s). It sold workout plans updated on subscription, with Notion templates for clients to track progress (Wiser Together EP.28). It was marketed on YouTube and Instagram and coached up to 15 clients at once (Epiphanies #2).
- **v1 numbers (Aug 2026 retelling, self-reported).** It grew out of a school product-launch exercise. Plans cost about ₹500 for two weeks and ₹1,000 for five weeks; 35 to 40 clients came from reels and referrals; each founder took home about ₹45,000 (roughly $500), which Moksh says he still keeps (9Ma2bozEqGM). "Up to 15 at once" and "35 to 40 in total" can both be true.
- **Why it died.** Messy systems, inexperience and burnout. Both founders burned out and shut it down. The lesson he took, in his words: "Good systems are the backbone of scaling anything." (Epiphanies #2). The Engine's named owners and fixed weeks read as a direct answer to that failure (inference).
- **First lesson in content economics.** He says Meteoric v1 taught him that social media creates leads and trust people will pay for (H68gyWOlIy4, Aug 2025).
- **Possible earlier trace.** A garbled caption in a Dec 2021 vlog has someone asking about Meteoric plans. Low confidence.
- **April 2025, the revival.** On a night walk in Sydney before his last lecture, he sketched the model, tried names like "Nirvana Studios" and "Blank Studios", found them generic, and went back to Meteoric because he felt emotionally tied to it. He was reading Hormozi's *$100M Offers* while shaping the offer (EP.60). In Oct 2025 he told the same story slightly differently: a late-night idea he could not sleep on, a walk, and a plan in his phone's notes app (target clients, income goals, team needs). He said he first planned it as a creative agency and paid a mentor early to move faster (no name or cost given) (Breaking down businesses, IEuERGHU3wA). This fits the May 2025 newsletter's "creative agency" framing.

### Operating rhythm and culture
- Remote by default; gathers in person at key moments (cabin build day, a Mumbai "founder house", team trips). Plans for team trips to Bali and Cape Town were mentioned for 2026 (nnMuox4BtbU).
- Twice-weekly team syncs (Jan 2026). A weekly ops and fulfillment call on Saturdays (Feb 2026). Discord and WhatsApp for communication.
- SOPs in Miro and Notion, metrics in spreadsheets. When something works, screenshot it, work out why, form a hypothesis, re-test, then delegate (n1vbE3e8XuE).
- Habits carried over from his PE boss: over-communicate, daily end-of-day updates, document every process so anyone can run it.
- Hiring: culture fit, ownership and intuition over raw skill, a rigorous application process, self-driven people who need little micromanagement. He says he has paid the team beyond what revenue justified in slow months and wants them to feel the company is theirs (nnMuox4BtbU, 9SBwERbSze4).
- Recurring signature line on culture: "Excellence is hygiene." (9SBwERbSze4): high output is the minimum, not a bonus.
- Tools named across sources: Notion, Miro, Google Sheets, Google Drive, WhatsApp, Discord, Calendly, Loom, Fathom, Claude (internal scripting workflow, with Claude Projects holding client context), n8n and Make.com (onboarding automation built by a friend, Oct 2025), Supabase and Vercel (site), Meta pixel, Whop.
- **Internal AI ("business brain").** In Aug 2026 Moksh said client Aryan Mahajan had just set up an AI context system for the company and taught him to treat AI as a living system that is structured and fed over time, rather than a dump of raw files. Moksh called engineering AI and context across the company his biggest lever (9Ma2bozEqGM). So part of Meteoric's internal AI setup came from a client.
- **Deep-work timing.** The highest-impact work happens late, around 11pm to midnight, with daytime going to clients and team. Said in the Aryan collab; which of the two led the point is unclear (9Ma2bozEqGM).
