# 02 Meteoric Boost

Index of everything about Meteoric Boost, Moksh Vasant's personal brand studio for founders: the company, its delivery method, its offer and sales process, its client cases and its messaging.

## Meteoric Boost in five lines
1. A personal brand studio (2025, run from India) that builds brands for founders earning $50K/month or more who are known in their industry but invisible outside it.
2. The 2026 offer is a 3-month done-for-you partnership run on the Meteoric Engine (5 stages, 14 phases, 12 weeks); the founder spends 4 to 8 hours a month on camera and does no homework.
3. The name comes from Moksh's 2020 calisthenics startup "Meteoric"; the studio grew from pre-sold friends in June 2025 to a claimed 80+ founders by 2026, with a small remote team led by Moksh (CEO) and Aditya "Adi" Vohra (COO).
4. Sales run through content, the Instagram profile, story sequences and DMs, then a qualifying call, a pre-sale video and a scripted closing call, largely handled by closer Arjun Sharin; pricing is value-based.
5. Positioning: recognition over reach, extraction over invention, taste as the deliverable, with Moksh's own brand and lifestyle as the main proof. All revenue and results figures are self-reported.
