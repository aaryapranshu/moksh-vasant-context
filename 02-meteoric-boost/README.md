# 02 Meteoric Boost

Index of everything about Meteoric Boost, Moksh Vasant's personal brand studio for founders: the company, its delivery method, its offer and sales process, its client cases, its testimonials and its messaging. Rebuilt on 2026-10-20 against full transcripts of 161 videos.

## Meteoric Boost in six lines
1. A personal brand studio (named April 2025, first clients June 2025, run from Mumbai) for founders earning $50K/month or more who are known in their industry but invisible outside it.
2. The 2026 offer is a 3-month done-for-you partnership on the Meteoric Engine (5 stages, 14 phases, 12 weeks): a 41 to 50 question extraction interview, an internal brainstorm, then production; the founder spends 4 to 8 hours a month on camera. In 2025 the same work was sold as "a coaching program" (done-with-you).
3. The name comes from Moksh's 2021 calisthenics startup "Meteoric" (Instagram @meteoricboost). The studio grew from pre-sold friends in June 2025 (Parmeet Kumar first) to a claimed 80+ founders by mid 2026, with a small remote team: Moksh (CEO), Adi (COO), the creative director (captioned "Modish"), and closer Arjun Sharin.
4. Sales run through content, the Instagram profile, story sequences and DMs, then a qualifying or audit call, a pre-sale video and a scripted closing call, largely closed by Arjun since Jan 2026; pricing is value-based and not public.
5. Positioning: rooms money can't buy, recognition over reach, trademark not niche, extraction over invention, taste as the deliverable, with Moksh's own brand as the main proof.
6. All revenue and results figures are self-reported. A private figure (₹40L+ a month) and the fact that Meteoric runs on FulcrumOS (Fulcrum Labs) come from Aarya, 20 Oct 2026; don't use the revenue figure publicly without Moksh's OK.

## Handling rules for this folder
- Every revenue figure, client count, close rate and client result is self-reported. Say so when repeating it.
- Several figures conflict (revenue, founder hours, client counts, client results). The files keep every version with its date and source; do not pick one, add them up or average them without Moksh.
- For client proof, follow [case-studies.md](case-studies.md) section 7: the client's own words first, never a title-only number.
- Client and team names from auto-captions or Whisper are approximate. Use names marked confirmed; mark others "captioned as". Confirm before naming anyone externally.
- Facts tagged [Aarya, 20 Oct 2026] are private context from Fulcrum Labs, not from Moksh's public content.
