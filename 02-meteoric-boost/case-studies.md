# Meteoric Boost case studies

One entry per Meteoric Boost client, reconciled across four kinds of source: the site's case cards, the testimonial videos on the second YouTube channel, the case-study and live-build videos on Moksh's main channel, and Moksh's retellings in podcasts and his July 2026 studio film. For each client: the confirmed name (or "captioned as"), industry, what Meteoric did, results by source with citations, and conflicts. It ends with a list of clients named only once and rules for using this proof.

**Read this first.** Every result here is self-reported, by the client, by Moksh, or by the company site. No metric has been independently confirmed. The site writes its cards in the third person and has no first-person quotes; the testimonial videos are the only places where clients state numbers in their own words, and even there Moksh often prompts with a figure first. Several clients sit inside Moksh's own network (a podcast host, his closer, a childhood friend, the host of a show he appeared on), so the proof is close to home, not arm's length. Names from auto-captions and Whisper are approximate unless marked confirmed.

Verification key:
- **SR-site**: self-reported on the Meteoric Boost site (company copy).
- **SR-client**: stated by the client on camera (testimonial or case-study video).
- **SR-Moksh**: stated by Moksh (videos, podcasts, studio film).
- **Partial**: some outside trace of the person or company exists, not of the result.

Per-testimonial detail lives in [testimonials/](testimonials/README.md). Rebuilt video breakdowns are in [../04-videos/](../04-videos/) and podcasts in [../05-appearances/](../05-appearances/).

## 7. How to use client proof

Rules for agents writing sales copy, decks, DMs or call prep.

### Proof tiers (strongest first)
1. **Client's own words on camera** (testimonial or case-study video, SR-client). Quote the client, name the video and date, keep "self-reported".
2. **Site card** (SR-site). The company's own copy. Use the site's wording and attribute it to Meteoric Boost.
3. **Moksh's retelling** (SR-Moksh). Use for colour, not for headline numbers. Where it is larger than the client's figure, use the client's figure.
4. **Titles.** Never use a number that appears only in a title.
