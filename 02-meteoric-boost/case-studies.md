# Meteoric Boost case studies

Every client case found across the Meteoric Boost site, Moksh's videos and his podcast appearances: who the client is, the archetype label used, where they started, what Meteoric did, the claimed result, the source, and how verified it is.

**Read this first.** Every result here is self-reported by Meteoric Boost or Moksh unless the row says otherwise. The site writes the results itself, in third person, and has no first-person client quotes. No metric has been independently confirmed. Several clients are inside Moksh's own network (one hosts a podcast he appeared on; one closes sales calls for Meteoric), so treat the proof as close-to-home, not arm's-length. Auto-captions garble names, so names from videos are approximate.

Verification key used below:
- **SR-site**: self-reported on the Meteoric Boost site.
- **SR-video**: self-reported by Moksh or the client in a video or podcast.
- **Partial**: some outside trace exists, but not of the claimed result.
- **Unmatched**: the person in a video can't be confidently tied to a named site client.

## 8. Patterns across the cases
- **Business outcomes lead, followers follow.** Cards open with ARR, inbound deals, closed revenue, investment, pricing power or show bookings. Follower counts come second.
- **Common time boxes:** 30 days (Aryan, Devesh, Daksh) and 60 days (both case-study video titles). The video bodies do not always support the title's time box.
- **Typical follower range for named clients:** about 9K to 28K, with outliers at 25K to 50K.
- **Low-follower wins** are used deliberately: $65K from one sequence at about 2,500 followers; offshore-dev consultancy signing clients with a tiny following.
- **Authority transfer** recurs: LinkedIn-first B2B founders moved to lifestyle-led Instagram.
- **Network overlap:** Parmeet Kumar (podcast host), Arjun Sharin (closer), the fitness friend (childhood friend), the first clients (pre-sold friends).

## 9. How to use these cases (for agents)
- Always attach "self-reported" when quoting a result to a prospect, and prefer the site's wording over video retellings.
- Do not quote the $3 to 4M single-video figure or the V3 Ventures investment as fact.
- Match by archetype first, then by goal (reach vs a niche of the right people, recognition vs revenue).
- For B2B prospects with small audiences, lead with the "Dish" and "Anus" style low-view cases and the presold podcast client.
- For young, wealthy, LinkedIn-first founders, lead with Aryan Mahajan.
- Confirm with Moksh before naming any client whose identity rests on a caption match.
