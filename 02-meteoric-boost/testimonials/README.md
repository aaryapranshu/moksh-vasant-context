## Patterns

### Who interviews, where, and how
- Moksh interviews every client himself. A team member appears once (the creative director, Modish, in the Karan video).
- Early ones (Oct to Nov 2025) are calls or simple sit-downs. From Dec 2025 they are filmed in person in aspirational settings: Dubai at the Burj Khalifa, a meal in Bombay, a client retreat on the Gili Islands, a Delhi hotel lobby full of art, a Gurgaon cafe after a Hyrox session. The setting itself is proof of the relationship.
- From May 2026 each video opens with a 55 to 75 second cold open: the client's sharpest "before" line, the headline numbers and one Moksh insight, cut together over music, before the greeting.
- The description CTA changed from Moksh's own Calendly (2025 to Jan 2026) to the closer's Calendly (arjun-meteoricboost, May 2026 on).
- Moksh restates almost every answer and adds his own principle. Much of the best Moksh material is in these restatements.

### The recurring questions (in usual order)
1. Where are we / welcome (gratitude, the trip, the meal).
2. Introduce yourself: what you do, who you serve.
3. Where were you before, and why did you want a personal brand?
4. Why did you reach out, and why Moksh over the other people you were talking to? (Asked in 8 of 9.)
5. What did we actually do: the process, systems, brand formula.
6. What was it like working with the team? (Adi and Modish named in most.)
7. The numbers: "walk the people through the numbers", "what's the big number looking like", "what's the delta". Moksh often prompts with a figure and the client corrects it.
8. The community (often added as "one more question I missed").
9. Accountability (Parmeet, Manav, Trisha).
10. What's next for the brand.
11. Closing question, almost word for word: "What would you say to someone who's on the fence of working with me?" (8 of 9; missing only in the short Kevil video.)

### Recurring result types
- **Business outcome first:** added ARR (Devesh), closed revenue (Manav), payment volume from one video (Kevil), bookings and price (Karan), lifetime revenue and deal size (Satvik).
- **Low-follower wins:** under 1,000 followers (Devesh), about 2,000 (Kevil), 6,200 (Trisha). The message: the right viewers matter more than reach.
- **Audience quality:** blue-tick mutuals (Umesh), an actor, galleries, PE people and an artist's grandson (Trisha).
- **Pricing power:** higher show fee (Karan), "charged premiumly" and doubled deal value (Satvik).
- **Low founder time:** under 2 hours a month (Kevil), 1 to 2 hours a week (Manav), 4 hours a week (Umesh).
- **Soft outcomes:** recognition on the street or at university, old contacts reaching out, confidence on camera, community.
- **Breakout first video:** about 100K views (Umesh), 10K (Manav), 70K on the second or third (Kevil).

### Title formula
Two eras.
- **Oct 2025 to Jan 2026:** `This [ROLE OR INDUSTRY IN CAPS] [closed / hit / built / found] [specific $ or metric] [with his PERSONAL BRAND / within a month / in just 2 months / with his FIRST REEL]`. Examples: "This FINTECH founder closed $50k worth of deals with his Personal Brand"; "This TECH CONSULTING business owner closed a $50K client within a month".
- **May 2026 on:** first person plural, prestige or outcome led: "How we built a personal brand for the VP of Goldman Sachs"; "We built her personal brand to attract billionaires!"; "We exploded this wellness coach's revenue & followers (case study)".

**Title vs content.** Titles often state numbers or claims the video never says: Kevil's "$50k", Tarun's "$100M+ net worth individuals in just 2 months", Parmeet's "8-fig", Devesh's "within a month", Umesh's "2.3k" (he says almost 2K), Trisha's "billionaires" (she says very high net worth individuals). The site cards sometimes carry the same figures. Treat a title number as packaging until a transcript or the client confirms it.
