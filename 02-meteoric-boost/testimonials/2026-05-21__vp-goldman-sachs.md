- **Positioning:** Moksh's "minor tweak": stop posting only DJ sets and song names, tell the story of a Goldman VP who DJs [08:32]-[08:57].
- **Ideas and tools, not done for him:** "they weren't doing the work for me they just told me they gave me the tools they gave me the ideas" [03:25]-[03:38].
- **Daily team contact:** "working with the team almost first couple of months every day almost" [05:54].
- **Modish** (creative director, based in Bangalore) helped with scripts and music; they shared rooms on the retreat [12:46]-[13:07].
- **AI tools:** "you've helped me a lot with AI as well" [16:36].
- **Offer:** Moksh is making Karan's planned info offer (teaching people to mix music) "my personal project" and says he'll be the first client [14:17]-[14:30].
