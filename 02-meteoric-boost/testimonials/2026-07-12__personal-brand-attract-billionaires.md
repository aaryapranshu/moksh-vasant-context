## Moksh's model in this video: old money vs new money
Generational wealth is quiet; new money is loud. New money eventually wants "more tasteful spending" to enter the circles it wants. So her videos let viewers feel they are learning "how do the elites think" [15:40]-[16:21]. This is Moksh's positioning logic for a luxury-niche brand.
