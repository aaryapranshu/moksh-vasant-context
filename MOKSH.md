## 3. What he believes

The 20 most load-bearing of his 122 mental models. Full models, evolution notes and host lines: [01-mental-models/](01-mental-models/README.md).

### Personal brand ([personal-brand.md](01-mental-models/personal-brand.md))
1. **The brand is the business.** Trust infrastructure that sells, hires and opens doors. "The personal brand is not a marketing channel. It is the business." (qSNYWCyKGyo [06:32])
2. **Recognition, not attention.** His founders are excellent and invisible. "It's better to be known by a thousand insane people than be known by a million people that will never matter to you" (w5ONkdV9OZw [03:01])
3. **Trademark, not niche.** A niche gets you compared; a trademark is yours alone. "A niche is a category... A trademark is a universe. Only you occupy it." (qSNYWCyKGyo [20:16])
4. **Extraction, not invention.** A costume brand shows. "90% of the work... is just of first extraction." (W3uYWLXJ4-A [09:12])
5. **AI makes the human the moat.** Strategy and tech are cheap now. "The real moat over here is human beings and personal brands." (Ze4JOAAvAq8 [05:45])
6. **Who watches beats how many.** Buyers watch silently and rarely like. "Market messaging trumps getting views and engagement, that is my contrary take on the industry." (Ze4JOAAvAq8 [12:12])
7. **Taste is the moat.** Restraint over flexing. "The actual top 1% does not care about you showing them that you're making a lot of money, but they care about taste" (HPCsujvgIvE [11:32])
8. **Stack credibility, don't argue it.** "You don't argue credibility. You simply stack it and display it in front of a cold audience." (0jNq7A4_yOM [12:27])

### Content ([content-and-distribution.md](01-mental-models/content-and-distribution.md))
9. **One format, one job.** Reach, leads or connection. "If you're trying to get reach while trying to get leads, you're not going to solve either problems." (qYIKwqRvIyU [30:47])
10. **Problems are the content, solutions are the offer.** List 10 to 20 ICP problems; each is a piece of content. "Pain point, value. Pain point, value. Pain point, value." (n1vbE3e8XuE [22:37])
11. **Any objection is a content failure.** Fix it upstream. "Any objection you hear on a sales call is a content failure." (qSNYWCyKGyo [29:05])
12. **Assets, not posts.** A good piece keeps selling while he is offline. "That is what an asset does. It works while you're not working." (M8TkhDOCZlE [05:56])

### Offers and sales ([offers-pricing-sales.md](01-mental-models/offers-pricing-sales.md))
13. **Price from the value of the problem.** About 5% of what the problem costs (n1vbE3e8XuE [43:46]). Anti-rule for copying market prices: "Do you have a brain or are you a goldfish?" (qmN62Kp4ohg [1:06:01])
14. **Price high, scale by price.** More clients means the team "will die in the fulfillment" (n1vbE3e8XuE [44:07]). "Charge as high ticket as you can and that is the best filtering mechanism to attract the best type of founders." (n1vbE3e8XuE [44:19])
15. **Give the method away, charge for implementation and access.** "Show 100% of your systems for free, charge for implementation." (qSNYWCyKGyo [16:49])

### Business ([business-and-operations.md](01-mental-models/business-and-operations.md))
16. **Act first, prove it twice, then systematize.** Dec 2025 view, replacing his 2024 "systems first": "Don't build systems first. Always take action first." (nnMuox4BtbU [04:07])
17. **Red is the to-do list.** Every sub-process is green, yellow or red on a Miro board. "What's red on the Miro board, that's your to-do list." (qSNYWCyKGyo [42:24])
18. **Don't fill gaps with your time.** Check whether a Loom, an SOP or a Claude project can do it first. "If you're doing something every week that someone else could do or own... that's a hiring decision you haven't made yet." (qSNYWCyKGyo [44:25])

### Mindset ([mindset-and-life.md](01-mental-models/mindset-and-life.md))
19. **Act like your future self.** Identity first, revenue follows. "I'm a big believer in acting like the person I know I want to be rather than acting like the person I am today." (n1vbE3e8XuE [40:12])
20. **Trying is action.** "The only way to know what the next 10 steps are is to take those 10 steps and let it reveal itself." (6SYJFqH-RPQ [35:30])

## 6. How he writes and speaks

Detail: [voice-and-language.md](03-content-playbook/voice-and-language.md), [titles-hooks-ctas.md](03-content-playbook/titles-hooks-ctas.md), [short-form-and-instagram.md](03-content-playbook/short-form-and-instagram.md).

### Voice rules
- Pick the register first: scripted solo (default), unscripted travel, live build ("bro", swearing), podcast guest, testimonial host.
- Second person to an already successful founder or operator. Never call them creators.
- Open on the claim, not a greeting. Proof before identity, then a humble turn ("It's not that I'm just bragging", nnMuox4BtbU [01:56]).
- "right?" as glue (377 times in 13 solo videos), plus "literally", "genuinely", "insane", sparingly.
- A number in every claim, then a time window. USD. British spelling in writing. Lowercase "i" in titles.
- Coin one label per section ("force versus power", "the range"). Credit people and books by name.
- Pair vulnerability with resolution. 2026 tone is calmer and taste-led than 2025.
- No em dashes, no generic AI phrasing: "It should not sound like AI. That's the whole point." (n1vbE3e8XuE [30:52])
