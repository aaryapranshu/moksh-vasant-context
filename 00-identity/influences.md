The books, people, thinkers and role models Moksh Vasant cites, what he takes from each, and where he said it.

Notes for use:
- Only influences Moksh himself named or discussed are listed as his. Ideas that podcast hosts raised are listed separately at the end so they are not misattributed.
- "Epiphanies #N" is his 2024 to 2025 newsletter ([epiphanies-newsletter.md](../06-writing/epiphanies-newsletter.md)); its text came through a summarizing fetch tool, so detail is paraphrase level.
- Podcast and video sources were read from auto-captions, which garble names. Spellings marked "(as transcribed)" need checking.
- For how these ideas show up in his thinking, see [01-mental-models/](../01-mental-models/README.md). For when he encountered them, see [timeline.md](timeline.md).

## Books

| Book | What he takes from it | Where he said it |
|---|---|---|
| **Psycho-Cybernetics**, Maxwell Maltz | Says it rewired how he relates to goals and "turned him into a winner". His summary: people are like bicycles and only stay balanced while moving toward a goal; restlessness and low moods come from not working toward the self-image you hold; live as your future self now; heal old "mental bruises"; use visualisation and meditation; know failure traits (fear, anger, uncertainty) from success traits (confidence, self-belief). He recommends it to anyone. The EP.60 host recalls Moksh talking about it mid-run at a Bondi half marathon. | Wiser Together EP.60 (recorded Dec 2025) and its show notes |
| **The Inner Game of Tennis**, W. Timothy Gallwey | Reading it in Dec 2025. Retells the story of a player who fixed his swing by watching himself in a mirror with curiosity instead of anger. Lesson: observe mistakes without judgment, do not ride the highs and lows, keep a steady state. He applied it to paying a fine that same morning without getting upset. He links it to his older Osho "watcher" practice. | EP.60 |
| **$100M Offers**, Alex Hormozi | Read it while shaping his offer in April 2025 (a research note says he read it in 2 days). Used it to define problem, solution, ideal customer and pricing, plus funnel and backend. His later view that price should follow value, not deliverables, sits in the same frame. | Epiphanies #13, #15; EP.60 (book list and the naming story); TSS #226 |
| **Essentialism**, Greg McKeown | The choose, discern, trade-off sequence. Feeds his "eliminate before you add" rule and his habit of cutting projects to protect focus. In Oct 2025 he applied it to the business: he cut systems, clients and one team member. | Epiphanies #10 (Mar 2025), echoed in #8 and #9; Breaking down businesses interview (Oct 2025) |
| **Mastery**, Robert Greene | Two ideas. "Rounded knowledge": he saw himself as strong on the art and emotional side and set out to build the analytical side (took numbers-heavy units, joined a consulting society). Later, letting deep work "breathe": tension comes before insight. He said in 2023 he was filming a book review of it. | "this self improvement theory transformed my life." (Jul 2023); Epiphanies #8 (Feb 2025) |
| **Atomic Habits**, James Clear | His entry point to daily reading during his late-2022 reset at home in India. Called it the best self-help book he had read: systems and daily habits beat goals, explained with bamboo-style compounding. | Wiser Together EP.28 (Oct 2023); "Why "self improvement" is ruining your life" (V6RXQ1_T_DA, Mar 2023) |
| **Deep Work** (author not named on camera) | Deep vs shallow work: phone on Do Not Disturb, expect discomfort before flow. | "stop negotiating with yourself." (hAYX4s6YbCU, May 2023) |
| *Discipline Is Destiny* (Ryan Holiday), *Meditations* (Marcus Aurelius), *The Courage to Be Disliked*, *The 48 Laws of Power* | His early-2023 reading list. Stoicism he credits with helping his cricket team win a tournament; *Courage to Be Disliked* with relationships and inferiority complexes; *48 Laws* as a way to spot manipulation, not a playbook. He read three books at once (finance, philosophy, random) to avoid an echo chamber. | V6RXQ1_T_DA (Mar 2023) |
| **Steal Like an Artist**, Austin Kleon | His Feb 2022 room was loosely based on it (he says the overlap was a coincidence he noticed later). Fits his later advice to copy the creators you love until your own style appears. | AVzCiJvNAI0 (Feb 2022) |
| **The Almanack of Naval Ravikant** | See Naval under Thinkers. Also credits it for his long-term "infinite games" thinking. One of four books he recommended in Oct 2025, with Mastery, Psycho-Cybernetics and Essentialism. | "Wisdom from a 19 year old boy" (Dec 2022); Breaking down businesses interview (IEuERGHU3wA, recorded Oct 2025) |
| **Expert Secrets**, Russell Brunson (the "attractive character" types) | Pick two of the four archetypes (leader, adventurer, reporter, reluctant hero) and hold them for about 18 months. His pair is adventurer (documenting the journey, for reach) plus leader (teaching the ideal client, for sales). | "How i made $52k in 3.5 months" (qYIKwqRvIyU, Sep 2025) |
| **The Startup Code**, Mukesh Bansal | Listed as a current read when he launched the newsletter. | Epiphanies #1 (Dec 2024) |
| An Osho book (research notes list *The Psychology of the Esoteric*) | Current read alongside the above; part of his ongoing Osho study. | Epiphanies #1 |
| **The Great Gatsby** | Mentions being on a Gatsby kick and asks readers for book recommendations. | Epiphanies #12 (Apr 2025) |
| *The VC Playbook*, *Stacking S-Curves* | Recommended during his private equity and venture phase (issue not pinned in research notes). | Epiphanies, spring 2025 |
| Harry Potter, Roald Dahl, Enid Blyton | Childhood reading. | Epiphanies #12 |
| *Relentless*, Tim Grover | The "cleaner" archetype (discipline over motivation), which he applied to Kohli. **Attribution contested**: a host introduced the book and Moksh engaged with it. | EP.28 |

## Thinkers and spiritual references

| Influence | What he takes from it | Where he said it |
|---|---|---|
| **Osho** | His father has followed Osho since the 1990s and introduced the family; Moksh first went to the Pune ashram as a small child and later did Kundalini, Dynamic and evening meditations. He sees Osho as an ordinary man who designed 100+ meditations, not as a guru. Core idea: watchfulness, being the witness rather than the body, mind or heart. At 19 he ended each day with a 10-minute Osho discourse and spent about a week at the ashram during his reset. Dates his first lasting watchful state to 2021. He used the watcher through his first marathon's pain. | "Wisdom from a 19 year old boy" (Dec 2022); EP.28; EP.52 |
| **Naval Ravikant** | Splits leverage into permissioned (capital, labour) and permissionless (code, media). Media needs no gatekeeper, which is the earliest form of his personal-brand thesis. He does not code but wanted to learn. | "Wisdom from a 19 year old boy" (Dec 2022) |
| **Lord Shiva (as an archetype)** | Detached, fierce energy behind his idea of being "ruthless" without being cruel. Calls himself spiritual, not religious; wears a mala as a reminder, chants and meditates. | "How i made 2025 my best year yet" (Dec 2025) |

## Family, as he has framed it publicly

- **Parents:** he credits their love for leaving him secure enough to face a harsh world. He has described his mother as unconditional belief and his father as high standards and tough love, plus the lesson to get back up stronger. His father also gave him meditation, which he calls his biggest gift. (EP.28; EP.52)
- **Grandfather:** scaled a logistics business over decades, which Moksh frames as his family's legacy-business roots (Oct 2025 interview). Died in April 2025, days before Moksh bet on himself with the launch program. Moksh wrote that he inherited his optimism, his love of building and of cricket. (Epiphanies #15; EP.60; IEuERGHU3wA)
- **Father's advice to stay present:** used as the family anchor in a 2023 essay on awareness (QtwotES26x8).

Attribution unclear (use with care): Roger Federer winning only slightly more than half his points, used to show that top performers treat setbacks as data. It comes up in EP.60 right after a host's point, and the captions do not make clear who said it.
