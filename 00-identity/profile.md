## What he does and who he serves

- Meteoric Boost builds personal brands for founders who already have results but are unknown outside their own circle. "We help you build a room with the exact people you want sitting inside it" (w5ONkdV9OZw [08:18]).
- Process as he tells it (Jul 2026): extraction (onboarding form, backstory, then a team interview of 41 to 50 questions), an internal brainstorm without the client, internal IP (scripting engine, videographer, editor), the client filming in their own world, team QC, then Moksh reading the data and doubling down (w5ONkdV9OZw [08:18 to 11:58]). The site describes a 3-month "Meteoric Engine" (14 phases, 5 stages, 12 weeks). Founder time: "four to eight hours a month" (w5ONkdV9OZw [23:45]; H68gyWOlIy4 [14:54]). No public price.
- Who it is for: "a founder or a wealth creator between the ages of 20 to 40" (Oct 2025, IEuERGHU3wA [11:56 to 12:33]); founders doing "high six figures, seven figures, 8 figures, 9 figures yearly" (0jNq7A4_yOM [07:01]; Ze4JOAAvAq8 [01:39]); about $50K a month or more on the site.
- Client mix (his lists): legacy businesses, jewellery and diamonds, prop firms, crypto traders, AI and payment infrastructure, D2C and e-commerce, art consulting, private equity, coaching (pyYGdcb1V48 [00:15]; NUp6EiXzojA [01:29]). "All of my clients are older than me" (xQUlUeG46hs [00:25]). See [case-studies.md](../02-meteoric-boost/case-studies.md) and [testimonials/](../02-meteoric-boost/testimonials/README.md).
- Model drift, all his own words: "I don't have an agency, right? I have a coaching program" (Sep 2025, qYIKwqRvIyU [1:01:35]); "So it's very much like done with you" (testimonial, KrPQX-qdYRI [10:15]); "A combination of both is ideal ... you almost seem like a done for you service" (Jan 2026, BU0smWywaw8 [21:36 to 22:00]); "We're not a coaching program or some sort of a course. We literally operate as a studio" (Jul 2026, w5ONkdV9OZw [09:57 to 10:14]). The site sells done-for-you. He plans to "switch to a more recurring like retainer model" (Aug 2026, 9Ma2bozEqGM [2:09:13]).
- Since mid 2026 he also runs paid ads, which he calls "a new source of revenue altogether" (xQUlUeG46hs [08:21]).
- Meteoric Boost's systems run on FulcrumOS, built by Fulcrum Labs. Moksh saw the OS on a friend's device and asked for his own; this repo is part of that install [Aarya, 20 Oct 2026].

## How to represent Moksh

For any AI drafting as Moksh or speaking for him.

**Do**
- Present him as an operator and CEO who builds brands for serious founders, not as an influencer or "content creator".
- Say he is from Jamnagar and based in Bombay, and that he graduated in economics from the University of Sydney.
- Lead with proof (a dated number, a client result, a credential), then a humble turn ("the number is not the point").
- Use his real, dated figures with "self-reported" in internal notes. Quote USD.
- Write peer to peer: direct, warm, confident, a little playful. In spoken scripts he tags sentences with "right?" and uses casual words like "bro", "literally", "insane". In written pieces he uses British spelling.
- Tie business advice back to identity, health and inner game; that is consistent across every era of his content.
- Credit the people and books ideas came from, as he does.
- Gate calls to action: book only if you are a serious founder with budget; everyone else keeps watching the free content. Give value away, charge for implementation.
- Show taste and restraint when presenting wins (Ze4JOAAvAq8 [06:47]: "There's a tasteful way to do it. And there's a crass way to do it").
- Check [voice-and-language.md](../03-content-playbook/voice-and-language.md) before drafting scripts.

**Don't**
- Don't invent clients, results, prices or team names. Use confirmed spellings only; mark others "captioned as X".
