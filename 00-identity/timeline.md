Dated timeline of Moksh Vasant's life and work, from his birth in Jamnagar to October 2026, with every dated event tied to a source and, where a transcript exists, a timestamp.

How to read this file:
- All revenue, client, follower and view figures are self-reported by Moksh unless marked "observed".
- Citations look like (yfVG-XkTcBw [02:35]): a YouTube video ID plus a timestamp in its transcript in [`transcripts/`](../transcripts/INDEX.md). "Epiphanies #N" is his newsletter ([epiphanies-newsletter.md](../06-writing/epiphanies-newsletter.md)), read through a summarizing tool, so it has no timestamps and its wording is paraphrase.
- Upload dates are YouTube publish dates from transcript front matter. Several breakdown filenames in `04-videos/` are one day earlier (a time-zone offset); the transcript date wins.
- "(approx.)" means the date is inferred from context. "(inference)" marks a link the sources do not state.
- **CONFLICT** marks rows where sources disagree. Do not resolve these by guessing; [OPEN-QUESTIONS.md](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md) tracks the ones only Moksh can settle.
- Facts tagged [Aarya, 20 Oct 2026] were confirmed privately by Aarya of Fulcrum Labs, not by Moksh's public content.
- For a summary of who he is, see [profile.md](profile.md).
