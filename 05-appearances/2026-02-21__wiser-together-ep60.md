# How he made $130,000 in 5 months at the age of 21 (Wiser Together EP.60)

Moksh's third Wiser Together appearance, recorded in Dubai the day after his 22nd birthday (mid-December 2025). It holds the fullest first-person founding story of Meteoric: the end of his Sydney private-equity internship, naming the company on a night walk, his grandfather's death, a programme bought with money he did not have, the first three clients (host Parmeet first), and about 35 clients and six figures five and a half months on. The second half is a reflective talk on burnout, *Psycho-Cybernetics*, *The Inner Game of Tennis*, action vs overthinking, the nervous system, and richest vs happiest.

**Context for this file:** Moksh Vasant is the founder of Meteoric Boost (he calls it "Meteoric" here), which helps "founders and wealth creators build, scale and monetize their personal brands" [03:46]. The host is a client, so this is not independent coverage. For the tactical playbook see [The Sanskar Show #226](2026-03-21__sanskar-show-226.md).

**Title variants:** the YouTube title now adds "| Moksh Vasant -" and writes "EP. 60"; the podcast title is "How he made $130,000 in 5 months at the age of 21". The description says he went "from finishing uni with no job lined up to building Meteoric, a personal brand agency serving 35+ clients and crossing the six-figure revenue mark" in "under six months".

**Revenue caveat:** "$130,000 in 5 months" is the show's packaging. Moksh never says $130K here. ## Key takeaways (Moksh's views only)

1. **External results lag internal growth.** 2025 was "a year that was in the making for ... at least 5 years"; the growth was "compounding over the last four years" and then "the external just explodes" [02:12 to 02:38].
2. **Teamwork gave him certainty, not skill alone.** From the StuKonnect launch: getting good at a skill is "just you working on something", but when "you can put a team together ... explain the vision ... motivate, inspire them" and "the data reflects that", you "viscerally have so much confidence" [05:49 to 06:14].
3. **Pick the model for cash flow and lifestyle.** "I'm young ... I want to live a good lifestyle ... build cash flow, and the best way to do that is through some sort of a service-based business" [08:14 to 08:26]. Looking at agency owners in his network: "I can do a 10 times better job than that" [08:48].
4. **Great, not best.** He turned down a referral to a bigger fund because he would make "a great analyst, not the best analyst" [09:22].
5. **Put a gun to your head.** He signed up for a mentor's programme he couldn't afford, on a payment plan, knowing the next payment was impossible unless he signed three clients [11:33 to 11:58].
6. **No regrets.** "There's no beauty in that ... You are who you are today because of the experience you've lived already" [15:49 to 16:02].
7. **Momentum is addictive.** "Momentum is very exhilarating ... and addicting", and you start to believe you only make progress when "all over the place" [16:50 to 17:17].
8. **Flow comes from structure.** For 2026: "a consistent sleep schedule, a consistent calendar ... planning things beforehand ... being more of a CEO than just a founder" [17:17 to 17:40].
9. **Every transformation unlocks a new self.** "I've gone through multiple transformations in my life ... every time you go through a transformation, you unlock a new self" [17:53].
10. **Money is a crutch until it's solved.** When young you say "I want to make money first" and fix health later; once money is solved, "life just shows you a mirror": dating, health, relationships, "moral character" [20:15 to 21:07].
11. **No financial goal for 2026.** "I'm not setting a financial goal for myself. It's very much what person do I need to be" [21:07 to 21:20]. (Conflicts with the "Road to $1 million in 2026" video posted on his channel the same day the episode went out.)
12. ***Psycho-Cybernetics* "turned me into a winner".** Winners and losers "does exist" even if he dislikes the boxes [23:17 to 23:42]. You're a bicycle that stays upright only when moving toward something [24:25]; depression comes when "we're not creatively working towards a version of ourselves that we've envisioned" [24:37]; live as that person "in the moment, very much present" [25:04].
13. **Zero feedback (*The Inner Game of Tennis*).** *Psycho-Cybernetics* is about positive and negative feedback; the tennis book is about "zero feedback" [26:38]: see the flaw with curiosity, no anger, no kudos [27:28 to 28:16]. "If you're too attached to the highs, then you're going to be super low" [29:02]; a "steady frequency ... is the most powerful state" [29:11].
14. **Action is the trying.** "A winner just tries again. But trying is actually action" [33:56]. Smart people "see 10 steps ahead and convince [themselves]" not to act [34:22]; "the only way to know what the next 10 steps are is to take those 10 steps and let it reveal itself" [35:30].
15. **It's impossible to fail with enough volume.** Even when one attempt fails, "those experiences and those skills are there to stay with you ... part of your war chest" [35:10 to 35:19].
16. **Regulate first.** Growth in fight-or-flight "will be out of pressure ... that growth isn't really growth, it breaks you inside"; from rest, "thoughts, feelings and emotions are expansive" [39:35 to 40:17]. "Abundance is the real key"; when abundant "you're just a magnet" [40:36 to 40:42].
17. **His only paid mentor is a mindset coach.** "Not a business coach, not a health coach, it's a mindset coach" [42:11 to 42:23]; rewiring takes a diagnosis and protocol, "at least 21 days" [43:16 to 43:28]. "Mentality is everything" [43:38].
18. **Frequency is contagious, and so are places.** Around someone in scarcity "it's a gear shift" [46:10 to 46:22]; in one city "growth is just natural", in another you work "against so much friction" [47:08 to 47:21].
19. **Everything is perception.** A 10K beside a steady partner is easy; beside someone crying at 3K it feels hard. "It's perception. Everything is perception" [47:30 to 48:11] (the host said "it's just perception" first).
20. **You can't be spiritual until the financial is solved.** "Most people would disagree with me on [this], but ... you can't be spiritual unless you've solved for the financial" [50:16 to 50:31].
21. **Happiest over richest; memories are the wealth.** "There's a finite few years you want to live"; richest but miserable has no point [51:45 to 51:56]. Time is "painted with memories" and "that's the most richness I've taken away from the year" [52:02 to 52:14].
