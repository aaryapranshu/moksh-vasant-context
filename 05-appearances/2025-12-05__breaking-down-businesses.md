# What It Takes to Build a Brand at 21 ft. Moksh Vasant (Meteoric)

## Key takeaways (Moksh's views only)
1. **The back end of a brand is sellable IP:** "the data that's tracked, the metrics ... the systems ... the team ... the aesthetics. At the end of the day, it's it's intellectual property" [01:15].
2. **Founders with good stories are invisible,** "leaving a lot of opportunities and money on the table" [01:46].
3. **Meteoric in three words:** "speed, systems and scale" [02:13].
4. **Action beats everything.** Every action produces positive or negative feedback; treat both as data. "Take dumb action if that's what it takes" [09:29].
5. **Product-market fit = one offer, one customer,** solve every problem so value is far above price and buying is "a no-brainer" [13:37].
6. **Price high.** A $10,000 client arrives committed; a $100 client brings limiting beliefs and wants a discount. Higher price, better clients, better results, more clients: "a virtuous cycle" [24:24].
7. **Culture and hunger over skill:** "skills can always be taught, culture can't always be taught, hunger can't always be taught" [23:29].
8. **Doubt and pressure are daily; pressure is good "as long as it it forces action"** [21:31].
9. **AI cannot replace a personal brand,** "a personal brand is personal for a reason" [26:36]. Use AI "only [in] the workflows that genuinely can benefit from AI", not out of fear [27:16].
10. **"Don't try to solve enterprise problems when you're starting out. Get so good at a skill that it is ... foolish to not pay you"** [28:45], then learn to market and sell it.
11. **Respect your context:** Indian founders from legacy family businesses are "born with operator genes"; don't measure yourself against Zuckerberg or Gates [29:13].
12. **Remove before you add** (Essentialism): cut systems, clients and people that weigh you down [35:24].
