## Key takeaways (Moksh's views only)

1. **Truth over ego.** "I want to find the truth, and if finding the truth involves breaking my ego multiple times, having my reality [shattered] multiple times ... that's what matters" [01:45].
2. **Gratitude from privilege.** "We come from very privileged backgrounds ... we haven't seen a lot of struggle", and acknowledging that "really puts you at ease" [04:24 to 04:37].
3. **Watch the loop.** Meditation separates "the watcher inside me" from body and mind, so in a negative loop "I'm able to watch the negative loop"; "you're not your thoughts at the end of the day" [06:27 to 06:52]. Bad loops are "inevitable"; what matters is "how quick you get out of it" [08:01].
4. **Script the identity every morning.** Meditate 10 to 15 minutes, visualize 5 minutes (character and goals), then review a list of traits kept on his phone: "I am not my thoughts but I'm present in the moment", "wherever I go, I go with me" (the watcher comes with him), "I act as if successes are inevitable" [07:21 to 07:47, 09:21].
5. **Meditate to switch contexts.** Between studying and the podcast he meditated in the cab so there is no "carryover from the past event" [08:40, 09:33].
6. **Comparison never fully goes away.** "I still compare myself, even this evening" [13:13]. It is "so subconscious" and depends on "what state you're in in life" [13:26].
7. **Design the environment.** Install Instagram only when posting; a separate account that follows only "good pages" [13:39]. As you grow you connect with people "you should not be following", and "if you're going for everything you're going for nothing" [13:53 to 14:19].
8. **Apps run on unpredictable reward.** "The one thing that we chase is reward and the app is just designed to reward you every now and then" [14:56 to 15:10].
9. **Discipline gives self-respect.** When disciplined, "the level of self-respect I have is immense and obviously that translates into everything that I do" [15:22 to 15:37].
10. **The vessel.** He takes no credit when kids from his hometown start running or posting: "I'm the vessel and someone, something or some energy is operating through me" [17:47 to 18:13].
11. **Live the chase while watching yourself.** The "average male experience" chases money, women, strength and a testosterone high; "I want to do all of those things ... but also just at the same time watch myself go through that path" [22:38 to 23:17].
12. **Overcoming, not achieving.** Chasing 10K Instagram followers, an internship or a marathon, the meaning sits in the moments "where you don't believe in yourself and then you prove yourself wrong" [25:26 to 25:40].
13. **If that person can do it, why can't I.** His role models are Virat Kohli and Iman Gadzhi; "I don't ask myself how or what ... I just ask myself if that person can do it why can I not do it" [36:47].
14. **Negative goal pictures come true.** Before the race: "if I have a negative goal picture in my head I am going to go through that negative goal picture", so he replaced it through meditation and visualization [42:03].
15. **Calm beats hype.** What works best mid-race is "putting myself in a calm state of mind" [45:12]; he enjoyed the marathon as "a fun Sunday" [45:27].
16. **Running is self-exploration.** "It's not about running ... it's more of a journey of self-exploration and your relationship with yourself" [47:39]. Power through pain once and "you can power through anything" [48:28].
17. **Adventure through responsibility.** "The only way to have a sense of adventure and enjoy life is to take more responsibility and scale high mountains" [52:05]; his next goal will need "a lot of character unlocks" [52:18].
18. **Knowledge vs experience.** He did Kundalini, Dynamic and evening meditations and heard discourses growing up, but only in 2021 did he reach a truly "watchful state" [56:20]. "You are your own home" [57:52].
19. **Convey the feeling.** On his videos: "the more I can convey exactly what I'm feeling, if I can make the person on a screen feel that ... I'll unlock a lot more success" [1:02:24].
20. **Build something useful instead of taking random sponsorships.** "I didn't want to work with random brands when I was making the content ... I wanted to have something that was truly helpful" [1:04:51].
