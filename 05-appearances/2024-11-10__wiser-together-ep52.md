# 5 Life Lessons We Wish We Knew at age 20 (Wiser Together EP.52)

Moksh's second Wiser Together appearance, at 20 and a month before his 21st birthday: a Gen Z and Millennial conversation about gratitude, social media comparison, his first marathon, how his parents shaped him, and running a many-fronts life through his own Notion system.

**Context for this file:** Moksh Vasant now runs Meteoric Boost, a personal brand studio for founders. In Nov 2024 he was a University of Sydney economics student, creator and template seller. This episode never mentions Meteoric, personal branding clients or an agency; the agency story first appears in [EP.60](2026-02-21__wiser-together-ep60.md). Its value is the systems layer of his thinking: identity scripting, environment design, and the marathon as proof.

**Title variants:** podcast title "5 Life Lessons We Wish We Knew at age 20"; YouTube title "GenZ And Millennial Talk About Life - Wiser Together EP. 52". Spotify's blurb oddly calls Moksh the host. Show notes list his links as instagram.com/moksh.vasant, mokshvasant.store and youtube.com/@mokshvasant.

## Key takeaways (Moksh's views only)
1. **Gratitude is easier from privilege but still takes steering.** He grants that a comfortable background makes gratitude easier, yet the mind still drifts toward threats and has to be pointed back on purpose (whether he keeps a formal gratitude practice is unclear).
2. **Meditate, visualize, then read your identity.** His 2024 morning stack (details below). One trait on his phone list sums up the frame: "I act as if successes are inevitable."
3. **You will fall into bad loops; the skill is getting out faster.** The watcher lets him notice a loop instead of becoming it.
4. **Comparison never fully goes away.** He admits he compared himself to others that same evening. It is mostly subconscious and tracks how he is doing in life. Social media is a tool, and how you use it decides what it does to you.
5. **Design the environment, not the willpower.** A separate Instagram account that follows only inspiring pages; the main app installed only when he needs to post.
6. **Discipline drives self-respect.** He feels most self-respecting when disciplined, and it shows in everything else. (The line that self-belief comes from keeping promises to yourself is probably the host's; Moksh agrees.)
7. **Overcoming beats achieving.** The win is beating your own doubt, not the trophy. The marathon is his proof, and the lesson carried over to an internship push and his business.
8. **The vessel model.** He does not take credit for others' progress. He sees himself as a vessel passing inspiration on, like dominoes, and points to young people from his home region who message him.
9. **Build useful tools, not random sponsorships.** This is why he turned his Notion system into a product.
10. **Balanced, multi-front growth.** He backs progress across university, business, training and inner life at once, which his template is built to run. (The "jack of all trades, master of none" framing is the host's.)
11. **Live the usual male drives while watching yourself** (attribution partly uncertain). He names money, women, strength and testosterone highs as the standard chase and wants to go through it while observing himself.
12. **Ambition is fine when it serves something bigger** (attribution partly uncertain). The idea that power matters more than money is the host's; Moksh adds that motive matters and leadership is good when it serves a broader cause.
13. **Contrast gives meaning** (attribution uncertain). Low periods often make sense later and can open something up.
