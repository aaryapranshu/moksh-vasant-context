## How to use this folder
- One file per episode, named `YYYY-MM-DD__show-slug.md` (date = public release date).
- Each file separates what Moksh said from what the host said. Only Moksh's statements should feed his mental models or voice.
- All revenue, follower and view numbers are self-reported by Moksh unless marked otherwise.
- Transcripts were auto-generated captions read through summarizing passes, so names are often garbled and speaker labels are approximate. Each file states its transcript access level.

## How his thinking evolved across appearances

| Theme | 19 (EP.28, Oct 2023) | 20 (EP.52, Nov 2024) | 21 to 22 (Breaking down businesses Oct 2025; EP.60 and TSS #226, Dec 2025 to Mar 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core story | Redemption: slump, the Virat Kohli moment, a habit reset | Systems: a daily stack, environment design, a template; the marathon as proof | Business: launching Meteoric, the instalment bet, first client on 8 June 2025, about 35 clients by Dec 2025 (self-reported) |
| Inner game | The watcher (Osho lineage), explained as his biggest gift | The watcher in use: during the marathon, to exit bad loops, in the morning stack | Zero-judgment observation from *The Inner Game of Tennis*; self-image work from *Psycho-Cybernetics*; a paid mindset coach |
| Slow foundations | Bamboo: years of roots, then a fast shoot | Template first named "bamboo brain" | External results lag internal growth: 2025 as years of inner work finally showing up |
| Accountability | Public: film the reel only if the routine is done | Relational: a candid friend critic, a trait list, discipline as self-respect | Operational: SOPs, metrics sheets, daily EOD updates, a team |
| Work | Past calisthenics startup "Meteoric" (v1); Bamboo Grow content | Jack of All Trades Notion template; internship hunt; no agency yet | Meteoric (Boost) as a personal brand studio; offer, leads, sales, fulfillment |
| Role models | Kohli, Casey Neistat, Tim Grover's ideas | Kohli plus Iman Gadzhi | Hormozi's *$100M Offers*, Iman Gadzhi (likely, captions garbled) as a model of trust and admiration |
| Money and goals | Vague big goals plus habits | Overcoming over achieving | No financial goal for 2026 in EP.60, with revenue as a by-product; happiness over riches. Conflicts with the "Road to $1 million in 2026" video posted the same day EP.60 was published, which names a $1M goal |

**Constants:** the watcher and non-judgmental self-observation; slow-foundation thinking; Kohli and running as reference points; Notion and systems as his tool of choice; the "Meteoric" name carried over from his 2020 calisthenics startup to the 2025 agency; comfort being open about low points and pairing each with a lesson.

**Shift in register:** at 19 and 20 he talks like a self-improvement creator (habits, meditation, identity). At 21 (Oct 2025, four months into the agency) he already talks like an operator building systems: departments, metrics, productizing, becoming a dashboard CEO. By 22 he splits into two modes: reflective and inner-game on Wiser Together (with the host supplying most of the metaphysical framing), and a number-dense operator on TSS, where he talks in formulas, checklists and funnel metrics.
