# Ultimate Life Lessons From A 19 Year Old Boy (Wiser Together EP.28)

Moksh's first podcast appearance, at 19, recorded at Parmeet's house in Sydney while he was an economics student there. He tells a redemption story: an overweight, bullied kid in Jamnagar, a first-semester slump in Sydney, and a reset sparked by a Virat Kohli innings at the MCG. Around it sit the ideas he still uses: the ripple effect, the watcher, bamboo-style slow growth, and filming his routine as an accountability device.

**Context for this file:** Moksh Vasant now runs Meteoric Boost, a personal brand studio for founders. In Oct 2023 he was a self-improvement creator. Nothing here is about the agency. The episode matters for the roots of his mindset and for the earliest known use of the name "Meteoric" (his calisthenics coaching startup).

## Key takeaways (Moksh's views only)

1. **Mindset is his differentiator.** Asked what sets him apart from other 19-year-olds: "has to be mindset number one, second has to be just having an indomitable spirit" [01:08]. The world is "super competitive and cold" and people fall off; a "bulletproof mind" gets you through [01:21].
2. **A loving home is the base.** Parents who raised him "with a lot of love" left him with "no insecurities, no trauma", so he can face the world [01:48].
3. **You are what you consume.** He curates books, music and podcasts (Joe Rogan, Wiser Together, Iman Gadzhi interviews) and keeps a self-improvement friend group that is about "polishing each other" [02:01 to 02:55].
4. **How you do one thing is how you do everything.** His ripple effect: fitness came first, then "fitness bled into academics, academics bled into content creation" [06:43 to 07:08].
5. **The comfort zone grows if you stay outside it.** Step out, "resist, persist" for a while, and the zone expands [07:34].
6. **The watcher.** His biggest gift from his parents is meditation and the "watcher inside", which he also calls spirit, "the god inside" or "the fabric of existence" [11:09]. Enlightenment is when "the watcher takes over" and you are "just a vessel" [13:11 to 13:37].
7. **Shrewd outside, pure inside.** After being taken advantage of in Sydney: "you have to be very shrewd on the outside ... and then you reserve your pureness inside for the people that deserve it" [19:01].
8. **Energy never lies.** Meditation means you know who you are, so you feel it when you act against that [22:10].
9. **18 to 25 is make-or-break.** "I feel like 18 to 25 is the age where you either make or break your life" [22:37].
10. **Intent and effort over outcome.** His number-one Kohli lesson: "if your intent is in the right place and you've given 100, 120% of your effort ... I don't see a reason why you should be worried about the outcome" [31:12]. Kohli is "not playing for stats" [31:40].
11. **Bamboo growth.** Five years of roots, then about 90 days to shoot up "8 feet, 6 feet"; outsiders call it overnight success [43:26 to 44:06]. The hidden work: small wins, daily habits, "distancing yourself from the wrong company", "having the courage to be alone", getting your intent right [44:19]. He took the bamboo idea from *Atomic Habits* [26:49].
12. **Influence by example, never by preaching.** "Most people do not want to really learn"; trying to convert friends never worked. He trusts that the right people come "in very unexpected and unplanned ways", and aims to vibrate at a frequency where a stranger leaves a conversation motivated [47:00 to 48:15].
13. **Film it only if you did it.** "Every time I want to have a good day I just film a bamboo grow [reel]" [52:00]. He does not fake his routine, so the camera forces him through it [52:27].
14. **Chase the stress.** The daily "why am I even doing this" moment (studying macroeconomics) is an opportunity: "the days that I don't get that stress is when I'm like what am I doing, I want that stress" [57:38 to 58:03].
15. **Creation mindset (probably Moksh, see walkthrough).** "I'm going to create something that didn't exist here before and I'm going to make this better. If you approach everything you do in life with that attitude I think there's nothing that can go wrong" [37:38].
16. **External pleasure has a cost (probably Moksh).** "Everything comes at a cost ... that thing holds power over you" [59:27]. He stopped eating chocolate and says he is now "addicted to" ticking off tasks [1:00:09].
