## Quick-reference cheat sheet

1. **Titles:** fully lowercase since 30 May 2026, lowercase "i" always, one hard number or status word, "personal brand" as the anchor, one parenthetical jab or tag at the end ("(your personal brand sucks)", "(unfiltered)", "(live breakdown)").
2. **Hook:** break the scroll in line one, land a hard number inside the first minute, stack 2 to 4 proof points fast, end on a countable promise.
3. **Credibility:** proof before identity, then downplay it ("the numbers aren't the point"), then the real reason. Restraint beats gloating.
4. **Body:** numbered points, each with a story, a number, the principle, why it works psychologically, and who it attracts. Add a caveat to strong advice.
5. **Retention:** number density, open loops, rhetorical questions, refrain lines, a new device every 60 to 90 seconds.
6. **Voice:** calm, confident, peer to peer, second person to an already successful founder or operator. "right?" as glue. USD, not rupees. "Assets", not "content". Hype and "bro" only in live or guest formats.
7. **CTA:** gated ("serious founders only", book from the description, otherwise keep watching free videos), or none at all on travel, case study and live videos. Never ask for likes or subscribes.
8. **Description (v3):** founder intro line, proof line, testimonials link, booking link, "Our work" link, Instagram, and the tagline about giving value free and charging for implementation, signed with the 🐐.
9. **What performs:** own-journey and own-numbers videos (about 14K average views) beat client case studies (about 3.4K) by roughly four to one. Case studies are sales collateral, not reach plays.
10. **Facts:** every revenue, follower and client figure is self-reported, and several conflict. Never invent or merge numbers; use [brackets] and ask Moksh.
