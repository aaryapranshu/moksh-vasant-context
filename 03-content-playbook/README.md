# Content playbook

How Moksh Vasant (founder and CEO of Meteoric Boost, a personal branding studio for founders) scripts, packages and voices his content, written so an AI can draft a new YouTube script, title, hook, reel or caption that sounds like him and follows his structures.

**Rebuilt 2026-10-20 from transcripts.** Every spoken line in these files is now quoted from a timestamped transcript in [transcripts/](../transcripts/INDEX.md), cited as (video ID [mm:ss]). Lines that turned out to be hosts', clients' or teammates' are removed and listed in [voice-and-language](voice-and-language.md) section 5.

**Typical load order for a new script:** script-template, then the matching type in youtube-script-playbook, then titles-hooks-ctas, then voice-and-language, then 2 or 3 similar videos from [04-videos](../04-videos/README.md) and their transcripts.

## Quick-reference cheat sheet

1. **Titles:** fully lowercase since 31 May 2026, lowercase "i" always, one hard number or status word, "personal brand" as the anchor, one parenthetical at the end. Make sure the video actually says what the title claims; several of his do not.
2. **Hook:** no greeting. Line one is a promise, contrast, pain mirror, claim or stat ("Anyone can get a million views, but not everyone can...", BU0 [00:00]). A hard number inside 15 seconds.
3. **Credibility:** proof before identity, then a downplay ("It's not that I'm just bragging", nnMu [01:56]). Restraint and taste over flexing.
4. **Body:** numbered or named points, each with a story, a number, the principle, the psychology and who it attracts.
5. **Retention:** number density, open loops, coined labels, direct address ("Let me guess"), a new section every 1.5 to 3 minutes in short videos.
6. **Voice:** second person to an already successful founder. "right?" constantly (377 times in 13 solo videos), plus "literally", "genuinely", "insane". USD. "bro" and swearing only in live and guest formats.
7. **CTA:** gated with a downsell to free content, two doors, or none on travel, case study and live videos. Never a like or subscribe ask.
8. **Description (Oct 2026):** "I'm Moksh, the founder of Meteoric Boost, a personal branding studio where we build the top 0.1% personal brands for Millionaires", booking link to meteoricboost.com (it was the closer's Calendly from Feb to Sep 2026, his own before that), "Our work" link to w5ONkdV9OZw, Instagram.
9. **Reels:** age or identity hook, a turn line, 3 to 5 concrete points, a punchline, optional comment-keyword CTA; 25 to 60 seconds at about 4 words a second.
10. **What performs:** own-journey videos (about 13,800 average views) beat client case studies (about 3,400) by about four to one.
11. **Facts:** every revenue, follower and client figure is self-reported, and several conflict. Never invent or merge numbers; use [brackets] and ask Moksh.

## Confidence and gaps
- Strong: every title and description (read exactly), opening lines and CTAs (verbatim from transcripts), section timings for all 21 founder-era long-form videos, the 32 scripted Shorts.
- Weaker: Instagram itself (no reel, caption, highlight or story could be viewed), thumbnails and on-screen text, and which Shorts match which Instagram reels.
- Auto-captions garble names and some numbers; spellings follow the repo where a match is confirmed.
