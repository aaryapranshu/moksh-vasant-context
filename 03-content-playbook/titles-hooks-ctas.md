# Titles, hooks and CTAs

The packaging Moksh Vasant (founder and CEO of Meteoric Boost, a personal branding studio for founders) uses on YouTube: his title formulas with every known title mapped, which titles claim things the video never says, his casing rules, the verbatim opening lines of his top videos, his reel hooks with counts, and his CTAs as spoken and as written, dated.

Titles are exact, including casing and punctuation. **Views are as of 2026-10-20** (from the transcript front matter in [transcripts/](../transcripts/INDEX.md)). Publish dates are the transcript dates, which are often one day later than this repo's file names (time zone). Revenue and follower numbers inside titles are Moksh's own claims, and several are not said in the video (section 3).

## 4. Casing and punctuation rules

1. **Lowercase "i" always in founder-era long-form**, including contractions: "How i made", "i'm just 22", "lessons i've learnt", "how i'd structure". Constant since Aug 2025.
2. **All lowercase since 31 May 2026** (from "don't build a personal brand in 2026 without watching this..."), including place and platform names (monaco, gstaad, instagram). Before that he capitalized "How" and some nouns ("Attracts Investors, Clients", "Millionaire's", "(LIVE)").
3. **One parenthetical at the end**, carrying one of: an objection-killer (without ads), (within 60 days); a format flag (LIVE), (live breakdown), (breakdown); a jab (your personal brand sucks); a candor or curiosity tease (hear me out...), (unfiltered), (here's why).
4. **Trailing ellipsis** for curiosity; **trailing full stop** for a calm declarative ("how to build a cult, not an audience.").
5. **Numbers numeric, "k" lowercase**: $52k, $130k, 130k, $1m, 22, 60 days, top 0.1%.
6. **"personal brand" is the anchor phrase** in 14 of the 21 founder-era titles (counting "Personal branding" in the collab). When it is missing a status word carries the hook (millionaire, top 0.1%, top 1%, India's best closer).
7. **Status vocabulary escalated**: 2025 titles speak to founders and his own journey; from Mar 2026, millionaire(s), multi-millionaires, top 1%, top 0.1%.
8. **No emojis, no hashtags, no ALL CAPS words** in founder-era main-channel titles (apart from "(LIVE)" before the lowercase switch). The testimonials channel's first era uses CAPS for the role ("This FINTECH founder"). Shorts used emojis on music-only edits.

### 6.3 Rules for writing his CTAs
- Gate, do not beg. Filter people out ("serious", "only for the top 0.1%", "Please don't go book a call").
- Pair a hard CTA with a downsell to free videos or Instagram.
- Never ask for likes or subscribes (no instance in any readable transcript of his own).
- Do not invent a budget threshold. Use qualifier words unless Moksh supplies a number.
- Unscripted and travel videos can end with no ask; the description carries it.
- For a new video, use the current description layout (Oct 2026: meteoricboost.com) unless Moksh says otherwise.
