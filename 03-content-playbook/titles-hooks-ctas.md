# Titles, hooks and CTAs

The packaging rules Moksh Vasant (founder of Meteoric Boost, a personal brand studio for founders) uses on YouTube: his 10 title formulas with every known title mapped, his casing rules, his hook formulas for long-form and reels, his CTA formulas, and 10 generated example titles for new topics.

Titles below are exact, including casing and punctuation. Views are from Oct 2026 (Piped listing, or RYD where marked) and are self-evidently approximate. Revenue and follower numbers inside titles are Moksh's own claims.

## 1. The 10 title formulas

| ID | Formula | Template | What makes it work |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Personal money proof | How i made $[X]k in [N] months through my personal brand ([constraint]) | A dollar figure plus a short window. The parenthesis kills an objection ("without ads") |
| B | Client case study | How i scaled a [age] year old [niche] millionaire's personal brand ([in/within] 60 days) | Young plus rich plus fast. Moksh stays the subject even though the client tells the story |
| C | Audience size plus outcomes | How i built a [N]k personal brand that Attracts [A], [B] & [C] | One hard number plus a triple benefit. Sells outcomes, not tactics |
| D | Public goal / road-to | Road to $[goal] in [year] (through my personal brand & [trend lever]) | An unresolved outcome the viewer can follow over time |
| E | Warning or contrarian claim | don't [do the thing] in [year] without watching this... / [common belief] don't equal [thing] (here's why) / how to build [X], not [Y]. | Fear of a mistake, or a belief the viewer holds, flipped |
| F | Age versus status identity | i'm just [age] and i work with [high-status group] to [service]. | Young and trusted by the rich. Reads like a text message |
| G | Audience plus location plus jab | for [audience], from [luxury location] ([blunt verdict]) | Filters the audience, signals the tier, and provokes in one line. A running series |
| H | Build or breakdown with a format tag | [How i built / Watch me build / how i'd structure] [asset] for [status person] ([LIVE / live breakdown / breakdown]) | Promises something tangible and tells the viewer the format up front |
| I | Reflective lessons with a candor tag | How i made [year] my best year yet (hear me out...) / lessons i've learnt [doing high-status thing] (unfiltered) | A personal verdict plus a parenthesis that pre-empts skepticism or promises honesty |
| J | Testimonial (testimonials channel only) | This [NICHE] founder built an [rule-breaking adverb] [adjective] personal brand with us | The client is the subject and Meteoric is "us". Hyperbole as the hook |

## 3. Casing and punctuation rules

1. **Lowercase "i" always**, including in contractions: "How i made", "i'm just 22", "lessons i've learnt", "how i'd structure". This has been constant since Aug 2025 and also shows up in his newsletter titles. Never capitalize it in a title written for him.
2. **All lowercase since 30 May 2026.** Every solo title from "don't build a personal brand in 2026 without watching this..." onward is fully lowercase, including place names (gstaad, monaco, oktoberfest) and platform names (instagram). Before that he capitalized "How" and some nouns ("Attracts Investors, Clients", "High Ticket Offer", "Millionaire's", "(LIVE)").
3. **One parenthetical at the end, lowercase in 2026.** It carries one of four things:
   - an objection-killer or constraint: (without ads), (in 60 days), (within 60 days)
   - a format flag: (LIVE), (live breakdown), (breakdown)
   - a jab at the viewer: (your personal brand sucks)
   - a candor or curiosity tease: (hear me out...), (unfiltered), (here's why)
4. **Trailing ellipsis** for curiosity: "(hear me out...)", "without watching this...".
5. **Trailing full stop** for a calm, declarative statement: "i'm just 22 and ... personal brands.", "how to build a cult, not an audience." Same device as his 2023 essays.
6. **Numbers stay numeric** and the "k" stays lowercase: $52k, $130k, 130k, 22, 24, 60 days, 2026, top 0.1%.
7. **"personal brand" is the anchor phrase.** It appears in most founder-era titles. He does not swap in "content", "Instagram growth" or "creator". When it is missing, a status word carries the hook instead (millionaire, top 0.1%, top 1%, India's best closer).
8. **Status vocabulary escalated over time.** 2025 titles speak to founders in general and to his own journey. From Mar 2026: millionaire(s), multi-millionaires, top 1%, top 0.1%.
9. **Youth as a multiplier.** His age (22) or the client's age (22, 24) sets up the young-and-trusted tension.
10. **Negative framing is newer and controlled.** Only in E and G titles. He never insults a named person, only the viewer's brand.
11. **No emojis, no hashtags, no ALL CAPS words** in founder-era titles (except the 2026 "(LIVE)" tag before the lowercase switch).

## 4. Hook formulas

### 4.1 Long-form (first 30 to 60 seconds)
Pick one. All of them must land a hard number in the first minute and end on a countable promise. Placeholders in brackets must be filled with true figures Moksh supplies.

| Formula | Fill-in pattern (paraphrased structure, not his wording) | Seen in |
|---|---|---|
| Proof stack | Lead with [headline result]. Add [3 to 5 quick proof points: followers, revenue, clients, ventures, investor interest]. Promise [the exact system / N lessons] in [time]. Then give your name | H68gyWOlIy4, nnMuox4BtbU, HPCsujvgIvE |
| Contrarian contrast | Getting [common outcome] is easy now; turning it into [rare outcome] is not. Then [your number]. Then "here's how it actually runs" | BU0smWywaw8 |
| Pain mirror | List what the viewer already tried [format 1, format 2, format 3], what they paid [agency fees], and who actually watches [wrong audience]. Diagnose the real gap. Then credentials and a numbered promise | Ze4JOAAvAq8 |
| Third-party cold open | Client or guest gives the before state and the after state, plus why doing it alone would have hurt. Moksh comes in second | sD4R8Z4H6U4, qYIKwqRvIyU |
| Situational drop-in | Start mid-scene in a specific place doing a specific task, with no intro. The "why are we here" is the hook | 9SBwERbSze4, qmN62Kp4ohg |
| Location plus open loop | Name the place, promise a story ("a deal that closed while I had no signal"), pay it off as proof a few minutes in | M8TkhDOCZlE |
| Stat plus self-test | A striking stat about first impressions, then a short test the viewer can run on themselves | 0jNq7A4_yOM |
| Payoff plus "my own playbook" | State the payoff a brand gives (premium pricing, inbound), then promise to break down his own brand as the worked example | NUp6EiXzojA |
| Future-self frame | "I'm documenting how I think at [age] so I can look back" | xQUlUeG46hs |

After the hook, his usual order is: proof, then a short downplay (the numbers are not the point), then the real reason or the setting.

### 4.2 Reels (first 1 to 3 seconds)
From his own explanation on The Sanskar Show #226 (Mar 2026) and his Sep 2025 workshop (qYIKwqRvIyU):
- **Age plus flex or identity.** Open with an age many viewers can project onto, plus a surprising capability. His own breakout reel opened by framing himself as a 20-year-old whose edge was clarity of thought (as he recounted it). In an A/B test he describes, a version using an older age underperformed, and dropping the age by one did better.
- **Vivid specific scene.** A precise time and place with high-status people in it (his example was a 4 a.m. hotel balcony with millionaires).
- **Conflict or negative angle.** A founder who does not look like their category, or a claim like "most creators are broke".
- **Unfair advantage on screen.** Lead with what is visibly true and rare about the person (a fitness coach leads with his physique).
- **Polarizing or surprising claim.** Something like a 21-year-old building a unicorn. Money flexes work for some people, not all.
- Production rules for the hook: good sunlight, face visible, a good outfit, a pattern-interrupt first visual. See [short-form-and-instagram](short-form-and-instagram.md).

## 5. CTA formulas

| CTA | Pattern (paraphrased) | Where and when |
|---|---|---|
| DM keyword "METEORIC" | DM the word METEORIC on Instagram to start a conversation | Spoken in H68 (Aug 2025); written in the qYI description (Sep 2025); a "DM me [keyword]" line also sits in his Instagram bio per BU0 |
| Serious founders only | Book a call from the description, but only if you are a serious founder or operator ready to invest; if not, do not book | H68, BU0, Ze4. Description wording in 2025: "For serious founders only" with a "Multi USD 4 figure Investment" line |
| Downsell to free content | If you are not ready yet, keep watching the free videos until you are | Paired with the gated call in H68 and BU0 |
| Calendly | v1 (2025): his own Calendly for a 1-on-1 fit chat. v2 and v3 (2026): "Speak to my team" or "get in touch", a 60-minute call on a team member's Calendly (calendly.com/arjun-meteoricboost) | Every description except nnMu |
| Exclusive strategy call | A call that screens for fit, pitched as for the top 0.1% | 0jNq7A4_yOM, HPCsujvgIvE (Calendly onboarding call) |
| Newsletter keyword | Comment a keyword (his was tied to the name of his Epiphanies newsletter) to receive the newsletter by DM, which builds the email list | His reel CTA as described in the Sep 2025 workshop. The newsletter has been silent since May 2025, so check before reusing |
| Instagram for the closer look | Follow on Instagram, where he documents his life more closely | Ze4 |
| Highlights as a sales page | Check the Start Here and Clients highlights | H68 |
| Limited spots | A capped intake mentioned inside the content (10 spots) | qmN (for the guest's offer) |
| Relational close | Thank viewers since the early days, welcome new ones, tease the next video | nnMu; no ask at all |
| No CTA | Let the proof carry the pitch; the description holds the link | Travel series (Monaco, Oktoberfest), case studies, vlogs, live builds |

Rules for writing his CTAs:
- Gate, do not beg. The CTA filters people out ("serious", "ready to invest", "not for everyone").
- Never ask for likes or subscribes in founder-era content.
- Do not invent a budget threshold. His spoken thresholds were inconsistent in Jan 2026 ($300 to $5,000, $3k to $5k, "multiple five figures"). Use qualifier words unless Moksh gives a number.
- Pair any hard CTA with a downsell (keep watching) or a soft relational line.
- The description tagline that backs every soft CTA: give the value away free, charge for implementation.
