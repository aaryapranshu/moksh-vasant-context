# Script template

Fill-in templates for writing new content in the style of Moksh Vasant (founder and CEO of Meteoric Boost, a personal branding studio for founders): two long-form YouTube shapes with timings taken from his real videos, a fully worked outline of his top video, a reel template built from his 32 scripted Shorts, a story-sequence add-on, and a review checklist.

**How to use**
- Pick the video type first. Structures and real timings per type are in [youtube-script-playbook](youtube-script-playbook.md) section 2; titles and verbatim hooks in [titles-hooks-ctas](titles-hooks-ctas.md); wording in [voice-and-language](voice-and-language.md).
- Everything in [square brackets] is a slot. Fill numbers, client details and stories only from what Moksh supplies or from this repo, and keep "self-reported" in your notes. If a slot has no source, leave the bracket in and flag it.
- He talks from notes in the scripted videos and with no notes in the walking ones ("I'm not going off a script over here", M8Tk [08:58]). So a script for him is a beat sheet with key lines, not teleprompter text.

## 1. Long-form templates

Timings are taken from his videos, scaled. Use template A for a 25 to 35 minute playbook, B for a 9 to 15 minute list or monologue.

### 0. Package (write before either template)
```
TITLE:        [lowercase, lowercase "i", one number or status word, "personal brand" or status anchor, one parenthetical]
FORMULA:      [A to K from titles-hooks-ctas.md]
TYPE:         [own-journey playbook | traits list | travel monologue | teaching breakdown | case study | live build | vlog | year review | studio pitch]
FUNNEL JOB:   [reach | positioning | authority | sales collateral | trust]
LOCATION:     [where it is filmed and why that place signals the right tier]
ONE VIEWER:   [the single founder this is for: revenue band, industry, what they have already tried]
PROMISE:      [countable: "the exact system", "six things", "in the next 30 minutes"]
PROOF BANK:   [3 to 8 numbers Moksh has stated that fit the topic, each with its source]
TITLE CHECK:  [is the title's claim actually said in the video? If not, get Moksh's sign-off]
```

| 2. Origin or model | 0:50 to 8:30 | 15 to 25% | [venture ladder: one rung per venture, each with a number and a screenshot] OR [the business model: DWY vs DFY, team, margins] |

| 5. Named framework | 16:30 to 26:00 | 30 to 40% | [4 to 7 named phases]. Per phase: [what it is] [his example] [a client or peer example] [the psychology]. One mid-roll pointer to the booking link around 16:30 |

## 3. Short-form reel template (25 to 60 seconds)

Built from the beat structure of his 32 scripted Shorts (Sep 2024 to Jun 2025, [shorts-2024-2025](../04-videos/shorts-2024-2025.md)) and his stated rules on The Sanskar Show #226 and in the Sep 2025 workshop. About 145 to 260 words at roughly 4 words a second, continuous voiceover, no pauses.

| Time | Beat | Slot | His verbatim model | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 s | Hook | [age plus identity / "I'm learning" / "I'm building"] OR [credential stack] OR [one-line thesis] OR [vivid scene with time and place] | "as a 20-year-old Clarity of thought is my superpower" (p6Qg2wproJo) | Movement or a zoom on the first word; sunlight, face visible, good outfit; lead with the person's unfair advantage |
| 3 to 6 s | Turn | ["And here's what I've realized" / "Let me explain" / "But, the real flex is"] | "And here's what I've realized." (kE2C3Lt5-QI) | Cut |
| 6 to 45 s | Body | [3 to 5 concrete practices, numbered, each with a precise specific] [optional named authority] | "Number one, maniac alert" (p7ZODv8gmiA) | Cut every 1 to 1.5 s; B-roll from work, lifestyle in motion, objects, POV, fitness; one grade |
| 45 to 55 s | Punchline | [a twist, joke or self-mocking line] | "valuations up only" (Eaewzy7bYgc) | Face or a clean frame |
| 55 to 60 s | CTA (optional; 7 of 32 had one) | ["Comment the word [WORD] and I'll send [resource] to your DMs"] or none on a reach reel | "Comment the word epiphanies and I'll send it over to you in your DMs." (qYI [21:26]) | End frame |

```
FORMAT JOB:  [reach (wide-TAM, B-roll plus voiceover plus house music) | leads (ICP problem-value) | connection (raw documentation)]
AUDIO:       [house track, 120 to 140 BPM per his Mar 2026 rule (TSS [18:01]); he said "180 to 140" in Aug 2025 (H68 [20:10])]
CAPTION:     [two to four short lines; open on the tension or result; one keyword CTA or none]
POST TIME:   [for an Indian audience, Fri to Sun around 8pm IST (qYI [54:55])]
FREQUENCY:   [at most twice a week (H68 [02:34])]
```
Guidance: write the hook three ways and pick the widest ("What's the widest total addressable market hook I can use?", TSS [16:25]). "If you're able to nail the hook in the first four to five clips, you have a video that will perform well" (TSS [31:01]). It must never sound like AI; read it aloud.

Founder-era adjustment: the Shorts were about his own life. For a founder client, swap the life practices for 3 to 5 problems of the ICP with one solution each ("Pain point, value. Pain point, value.", TSS [22:37]).

### Story-sequence add-on

Frame count changed over time: 6 to 7, "won't go over seven" (Sep 2025, qYI [49:34]); 9 frames built live (Oct 2025, wi6p); "eight to 12 stories" (Mar 2026, TSS [23:17]). Pick by audience and say which rule you used.

```
1. [pattern interrupt: aesthetic photo or video, no text]
2. [call out the ICP: who they are, what they are building]
3. [current-state pain, praising and needling at once]
4. [transition: "Picture this real quick"]
5. [dream outcome, specific]
6. [case study: a named client result]
7. [proof detail in affluent vocabulary]
8. [the pain again]
9. [scarcity (only if true) plus keyword CTA: "DM me the word [WORD]"]
```
This is the 9-frame Peak Form arc (wi6p [57:53] to [1:02:48]). For a 6 to 7 frame version, merge 2 and 3, and drop 7 and 8. Curiosity sequences can run without a CTA; he rations CTAs to about two a month (qYI [49:09] to [49:22]).

## 4. Review checklist

**Package**
- [ ] Title fully lowercase, lowercase "i", one parenthetical at the end, no emojis
- [ ] One hard number or one status word, and "personal brand" or a status anchor
- [ ] The title's claim is said in the video, or Moksh has approved it
- [ ] The type matches the funnel job (case studies are sales collateral)

**Hook and credibility**
- [ ] No greeting; line one is a promise, contrast, pain mirror, claim, stat or third-party voice
- [ ] A hard number inside 15 seconds
- [ ] Proof before his name; a downplay after the stack
- [ ] The promise is countable

**Body**
- [ ] Each point has a story, a number, a principle, the psychology and who it attracts
- [ ] A new section or device every 1.5 to 3 min (short videos) or 3 to 5 min (playbooks)
- [ ] Strong advice carries a caveat; any struggle is resolved
- [ ] Location and lifestyle prove a point; no gloating

**Voice**
- [ ] Second person, to a founder or operator
- [ ] "right?" frequent but not every line; "literally", "genuinely", "insane" used lightly
- [ ] No line from voice-and-language.md section 5 (lines that are not his)
- [ ] No "game changer", "hey guys", "influencer" as a positive, no em dashes, no AI filler
- [ ] USD and British spelling

**Facts**
- [ ] Every number traces to Moksh or this repo, or is a bracketed placeholder
- [ ] Conflicting figures not merged or rounded
- [ ] No invented client names, quotes or results

**Close**
- [ ] CTA matches the type (gated, two doors, none, or relational); no like or subscribe ask
- [ ] Description follows the current layout
- [ ] Flagged for Moksh's review before publishing
