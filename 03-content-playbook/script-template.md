# Script template

Fill-in-the-blanks templates for writing new content in the style of Moksh Vasant (founder of Meteoric Boost, a personal brand studio for founders): a long-form YouTube script with section-by-section guidance and timing, a 60-second reel, and a checklist to review any draft against his patterns.

**How to use**
- Pick the video type first. The structures behind this template are in [youtube-script-playbook](youtube-script-playbook.md); titles in [titles-hooks-ctas](titles-hooks-ctas.md); wording in [voice-and-language](voice-and-language.md).
- Everything in [square brackets] is a slot. Fill numbers, client details and stories only from what Moksh supplies or from this repo, and keep "self-reported" in your notes. If a slot has no source, leave the bracket in and flag it.
- Write in his scripted-solo register (calm, confident, second person) unless it is a live build or a guest conversation.
- He works from notes and talks loosely, so a script for him is a beat sheet with key lines, not a word-for-word teleprompter text.

## 1. Long-form YouTube template

Default length: 12 to 18 minutes (his traits and travel videos run 9 to 15; teaching breakdowns 18 to 36; own-journey playbooks 31 to 35). Timings below assume 15 minutes. For a 30-minute playbook, double the body sections and add sections 4b and 4c.

### 0. Package (write before the script)
```
TITLE:        [lowercase, lowercase "i", one number or status word, "personal brand" or status anchor, one parenthetical]
FORMULA:      [A to I from titles-hooks-ctas.md]
TYPE:         [own-journey playbook | traits list | travel monologue | teaching breakdown | case study | live build | vlog | year review]
FUNNEL JOB:   [reach | positioning | authority | sales collateral | trust]
LOCATION:     [where it is filmed, and why that place signals the right tier]
ONE VIEWER:   [the single founder this is for: revenue band, industry, what they have already tried]
PROMISE:      [countable: "six things", "the exact system", "12 lessons", "how my own brand does it"]
PROOF BANK:   [3 to 7 numbers Moksh has stated that are relevant to this topic]
```

### 1. Hook (0:00 to 0:45)
Pick one archetype: proof stack, contrarian contrast, pain mirror, third-party cold open, situational drop-in, location plus open loop, stat plus self-test.
```
LINE 1 (scroll break):   [the contrarian claim | the list of what they already tried | the place plus the story you will tell]
HEADLINE NUMBER:         [one hard figure in the first 20 seconds]
PROOF POINTS:            [2 to 4 fast ones: founders worked with, client revenue band, a turned-down offer, followers]
PROMISE:                 [the countable payoff]
```
Guidance: no "hey guys, welcome back". No name yet if the hook is a proof stack. The first line should make a successful founder feel seen or challenged.

### 2. Who's talking and why now (0:45 to 1:30)
```
NAME AND ROLE:      [I'm Moksh, I run Meteoric Boost, a personal branding studio for ...]
DOWNPLAY:           [the numbers are not the point; here is the real reason]
SETTING:            [where we are and why I wrote this here]
WHY NOW:            [the stake: AI makes tools and strategy cheap, so the person is the moat | a trend the viewer is feeling]
```
Guidance: this is where his restraint shows. Context, not a flex. One sentence on the setting is enough.

### 3. Diagnosis (1:30 to 2:30)
```
WHAT THEY THINK THE PROBLEM IS:   [views, editing, camera confidence, the wrong agency]
WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS:              [substance | positioning | legibility | the wrong audience]
COST OF NOT FIXING IT:            [lost deals, no inbound, priced like everyone else]
```

### 4. Body: N points (2:30 to 12:30, about 90 to 120 seconds each for 5 to 6 points)
Repeat this block per point.
```
POINT [n]: [short name for the principle]
  STORY:         [a personal or client moment with one real number]
  PRINCIPLE:     [what it shows, in one plain sentence]
  WHY IT WORKS:  [the psychology: recognition, aspiration, FOMO, trust, status]
  WHO IT PULLS:  [which kind of person this attracts, or "this is you if..."]
  CAVEAT:        [the nuance that stops it being bad advice]
  DEVICE:        [one retention device: open loop, rhetorical question, refrain line, numbered checkpoint, audio cue]
```
Guidance:
- Every 60 to 90 seconds there should be a new device.
- Mix one long explanation with one short punchy line per point.
- "right?" at most two or three times per point.
- If a point has no number, it is not finished.
- He sometimes loses count of his list on camera. Do not script that, but do not over-polish a natural slip out of the edit either.

For a 30-minute own-journey playbook add:
```
4b. ORIGIN LADDER (before the points, 4 to 7 min): [2017 camera] -> [2020 calisthenics startup] -> [Notion templates] -> [StuKonnect] -> [the content breakthrough] -> [Meteoric]. One number and one screenshot per rung.
4c. OPS LAYER (inside the points): [team roles], [tools], [weekly review], [founder hours per month]
```
For a live build, replace section 4 with the offer fields in order: name, thesis, segmentation, headline (made conditional), ICP, pains with cost in hours and dollars, before and after, unfair advantage, proof, conservative timeline, fulfillment, funnel, price from value math, capacity, launch assets.

### 5. Reframe and recap (12:30 to 13:45)
```
QUOTABLE REFRAME:   [one line that sticks: assets, not content | views don't matter | the right 15 viewers]
RECAP:              [the N points in one breath each]
```

### 6. Vision beat (13:45 to 14:15)
```
STAKE:   [AI makes people replaceable, a brand does not | a public goal with a date | thanks to old and new viewers]
```

### 7. CTA and sign-off (14:15 to 15:00)
Pick by type (see the CTA table in youtube-script-playbook.md):
```
GATED:      [the call link is in the description, but only for serious founders/operators who are ready; if that's not you yet, keep watching the free videos]
SECONDARY:  [Instagram, where I document my life more closely]
OR NONE:    [travel monologues, case studies and live builds can end without an ask]
SIGN-OFF:   [from the location; casual; one line]
```
Do not add a like or subscribe request.

### 8. Description (v3 layout)
```
I'm Moksh, founder of Meteoric Boost, a personal branding studio [for millionaires / for founders].
[Proof line Moksh approves, e.g. brands scaled in the past year]
Testimonials: [youtube.com/@mokshvasanttestimonials]
Book a call: [team Calendly link]
Our work: [youtu.be/w5ONkdV9OZw]
Instagram: [@moksh.vasant]
We give away all the value for free & charge for the implementation, The Meteoric Boost way 🐐
```
No chapters, few tags (topic, location, his name, the status word).

## 2. Short-form reel template (about 60 seconds)

Based on his stated reel formula (The Sanskar Show #226, Mar 2026) and his Hook, Authority, Flow, CTA skeleton (Sep 2025 workshop). See [short-form-and-instagram](short-form-and-instagram.md).

| Time | Beat | Slot | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3s | Hook | [age plus identity or flex] OR [vivid specific scene with a time and place] OR [polarizing claim] | Pattern-interrupt first shot: movement, zoom or a cut on the first word. Sunlight, face visible, good outfit |
| 3 to 8s | Authority | [one credibility number: founders, revenue band, followers] | A proof shot or a status shot (work, travel, team) |
| 8 to 50s | Flow | [the story or lesson in 4 to 6 short lines; one turn or surprise in the middle] | Cut every 1 to 1.5 seconds. B-roll from his categories: work, screens, lifestyle in motion, objects, POV, fitness. One grade for the whole reel |
| 50 to 60s | CTA | [comment or DM a keyword] OR [no CTA for a reach reel] | Face to camera or a clean end frame |

```
FORMAT JOB:  [reach (B-roll plus voiceover plus house music) | leads (ICP value / whiteboard) | connection (raw talk to camera) | fun (edit)]
AUDIO:       [house track around 120 to 140 BPM that builds]
CAPTION:     [two to four short lines; lowercase "i"; open on the tension or result; one keyword CTA or none]
POST TIME:   [about 30 min before audience peak; for an Indian audience, Fri to Sun around 8pm IST per his 2025 workshop]
```
Guidance: the first four or five clips decide the reel. Write the hook three ways and pick the broadest one. Scripts must never sound like AI; read it aloud in his voice before handing it over.

### Story sequence add-on (8 to 12 frames; or 6 to 7 per his earlier advice)
```
1. [pattern interrupt]
2. [identity gap: where they are versus who they want to be]
3 to 5. [the dream outcome, made specific]
6 to 8. [proof: a client result or his own numbers]
9. [scarcity: limited slots]
10. [keyword CTA: DM [WORD]]
```
Every sequence ends with a CTA. Credibility-heavy sequences earned the most for him (self-reported).

## 3. Review checklist

Run every draft against this before it goes to Moksh.

**Package**
- [ ] Title fully lowercase, lowercase "i", one parenthetical at the end, no emojis
- [ ] Title has one hard number or one status word, and "personal brand" or a status anchor
- [ ] The type matches the funnel job (case studies are sales collateral, not reach plays)

**Hook and credibility**
- [ ] First line breaks the scroll (contrarian, pain mirror, scene, or third-party voice)
- [ ] A hard number lands in the first minute
- [ ] Proof comes before his name; a downplay follows the proof stack
- [ ] The promise is countable

**Body**
- [ ] Each point has a story, a number, a principle, a why, and who it attracts
- [ ] A retention device roughly every 60 to 90 seconds
- [ ] Strong advice carries a caveat
- [ ] Any struggle shared is resolved into a lesson
- [ ] Location and lifestyle prove a point; no gloating

**Voice**
- [ ] Second person, to a founder or operator, never "creators"
- [ ] USD, British spelling, "assets" not "content" (2026 framing)
- [ ] "right?" and intensifiers used lightly
- [ ] No AI filler words, no em dashes, no hype without a number
- [ ] Hype register only in live or guest formats

**Facts**
- [ ] Every number traces to Moksh or this repo, or is a bracketed placeholder
- [ ] Conflicting revenue figures are not merged or rounded into a new claim
- [ ] No invented client names, quotes or results
- [ ] No private details beyond what he has shared publicly

**Close**
- [ ] CTA matches the type (gated, none, or relational); no like or subscribe ask
- [ ] Description follows the v3 layout with the 🐐 tagline
- [ ] Flagged for Moksh's review before publishing
