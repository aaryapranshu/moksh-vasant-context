# Offers, pricing and sales: how Moksh thinks

Moksh builds an offer as a fixed list of decisions, prices it from what the buyer's problem costs, sells it through Instagram DMs and a short sequence of calls, and lets client results do the marketing.

Context for any agent loading only this file: Moksh Vasant (born in Jamnagar, turned 21 in December 2024) runs Meteoric Boost, a personal brand studio for founders, started in 2025. Every revenue, price, close-rate and client figure below is **self-reported and unaudited**. Several conflict with each other; see [OPEN-QUESTIONS.md](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md).

## Top 5 in one line each
1. **Price from the value of the problem:** charge about 5% of what the problem costs the buyer, never a price set by deliverables or gut feel.
2. **An offer is a document of decisions:** name, thesis, headline, ICP, pains, before and after, edge, proof, fulfillment, funnel, price, capacity, launch kit. Fill in every field before launch.
3. **DM is the funnel:** content, then the profile, a story sequence, a keyword DM, a setter, a short qualifying call, a pre-sale video, then a one-call close.
4. **Filter hard:** high prices, budget gates and "serious founders only" calls to action keep the wrong buyers off the calendar.
5. **Client proof is the marketing:** every result becomes a testimonial, case study or story sequence that sells the next client.

### 1. Price from the value of the problem
**The idea:** A price comes from what the buyer's problem costs them, not from what you deliver or what feels right. Work out the cost, charge a small slice of it, and make the return obvious.
**How he applies it:**
- Estimate the yearly cost of the problem (his example: about Rs 1 crore of lost leads in a year), then charge about 5% of it.
- Put a dollar value on the buyer's time. In the Dialed session: an owner making $20k a month works about 120 hours, so each hour is worth about $166, and 20 hours a month spent closing costs about $3.3k.
- Add the time value to the revenue the solution produces, take a small share, then turn it into a rule the client can repeat. For Arjun Sharin's B2B closer service, the rule was that the setup fee equals the owner's own offer price.
- Anchor a training program to the buyer's first month of earnings, so it pays for itself in under a month. Dialed's closer program was about $500, against at least about $1,000 a month in commission. It was also quoted as about 45,000 INR, which does not match at normal exchange rates.
- Call the price an investment, not a fee.
- Raise prices as results, close rate and demand go up. In the Dialed build, a steady 50% close rate was the trigger.
**Where he said it:** The Sanskar Show #226, pricing chapter (21 Mar 2026). High Ticket Offer LIVE (qmN62Kp4ohg, 21 Mar 2026). Build a brand from scratch LIVE (wi6pyq5nXK8, about Nov 2025): about $1,500 for four months, rising as results come in. Breaking down businesses (recorded 9 Oct 2025): prices rise with results.
**Evolution / confidence:** High on the method, low on the arithmetic. The live Dialed math doesn't add up as spoken. 5% of the stated $171k total is about $8.5k, not the "85,000" in the transcript, and subtracting commission doesn't give the stated result. Reuse the method and the rule, never those numbers. His own ticket has moved over time:
- 2025 descriptions: a multi-thousand-dollar investment, for serious founders only.
- Jan 2026: $3k to $7k, with typical closes of about $5k to $6k.
- Oct 2026: clients pay about 3x what competitors charge.

All of these are self-reported. The current fee is not public.

### 2. An offer is a fixed sequence of decisions (the Dialed build)
**The idea:** An offer is built, not made up. Every offer goes through the same fields in the same order, and nothing launches until each field has an answer.
**How he applies it:** The order he worked through live for Dialed:
0. **Credibility first.** Establish the operator's proof, because the offer rests on it.
1. **Profile audit.** Does the bio say who you help, how, and with what results?
2. **Name.** Short and tied to the operator's identity ("Dialed" means locked in).
3. **Thesis.** Which two trends meet, and where is the gap?
4. **Segmentation.** One side or two? B2B and B2C can run off the same content.
5. **Headline.** One sentence, repeated until it is concrete. Then add the condition that makes it true: a 50%+ close rate holds only if the owner already generates leads.
6. **ICP.** Gender, age band, niche, income floor (for example $10k a month). Is the ICP a past version of the operator?
7. **Pains and the cost of doing nothing,** forced into hours and money.
8. **Three before points** for the buyer's life today.
9. **Three after points** for the buyer's life once it works.
10. **Competitive edge.** He turns the operator's lifestyle into the unfair advantage.
11. **Pain to deliverable.** Map each pain to something delivered.
12. **Proof inventory.**
13. **Timeline promise.** Always the most conservative number: 2 months, not 2 weeks.
14. **Fulfillment.**
15. **Funnel.**
16. **Price** from value math (model 1).
17. **Capacity cap** (model 21).
18. **Smallest launch kit:** a story sequence, one long-form video, 2 to 3 testimonials, a pre-sale asset, and a Calendly link.

Other rules from his builds:
- Pick one outcome market: health, wealth or relationships.
- Define point A, point B and the ICP.
- Stack about 13 to 16 pains, each paired with a fix, so perceived value clearly beats the price.
- Define the dream outcome in physical, mental and social terms.
- Know the ICP down to their daily routine, delegation habits, fears and what they are proud of. Build avatar pictures so you can speak to them directly.
**Where he said it:** High Ticket Offer LIVE (21 Mar 2026). Build a brand from scratch LIVE (about Nov 2025). $130k in 6 months (BU0smWywaw8, 12 Jan 2026). The Sanskar Show #226 (Mar 2026). The root is Hormozi's *$100M Offers*, which he read while shaping his first agency offer in April 2025 (Wiser Together EP.60; Epiphanies #13 and #15).
**Evolution / confidence:** High. The same skeleton shows up in two live builds about four months apart. The numbered list is reconstructed from the order he worked in, not a document he published. The Dialed transcript has no speaker labels, so a few lines may be Arjun's.

### 3. DM is the only funnel (the profile funnel)
**The idea:** A conversation replaces the landing page. Each step moves a stranger one stage closer to a call, and the sale happens through a DM thread, not a website. His line: "DM is the only funnel." (Jan 2026).
**How he applies it:** The path, in order:
1. Short-form reach.
2. The optimized Instagram profile, which works as the landing page.
3. Long-form YouTube for authority, with testimonials inside it.
4. A story sequence. There are four types (CTA or value, credibility, results, curiosity), and each ends with a CTA.
5. A keyword DM (for example "METEORIC").
6. An appointment setter qualifies the prospect.
7. A Calendly quiz.
8. A short qualifying call (model 10).
9. A pre-sale video (model 9).
10. A one-call close (model 7).
11. Onboarding, a quick win, a testimonial, then an upsell.

He tracks every sequence for replies, booked calls, closes and drop-off. By his account, credibility sequences earn the most, and single sequences have brought in about $3k to $35k each. He also says a client with about 2,500 followers closed $65K from one sequence his team built.
**Where he said it:** $130k in 6 months (12 Jan 2026). High Ticket Offer LIVE (21 Mar 2026). Breaking down businesses (Oct 2025): story sequence, keyword DM, setter, discovery call, closing call. The Sanskar Show #226 (Mar 2026). Build a brand from scratch LIVE (about Nov 2025).
**Evolution / confidence:** High for 2025 to early 2026. Two later changes:
- In Jan 2026 he said: no website and no VSL. By about Sep 2026, his profile breakdown (0jNq7A4_yOM) puts a link to a pre-sell video in the bio, which he calls a VSL.
- The 2026 studio site runs an apply form, with a Meta pixel firing on completed applications. That suggests paid traffic (an inference, not something he said).

The DM path is still the core, but DM-only describes the earlier system. There is also an open conflict on story-sequence length: 8 to 12 frames (The Sanskar Show, Mar 2026) vs a cap of 6 or 7 frames because drop-off compounds (Sep 2025 workshop).

### 4. Filter for serious buyers before they reach a call
**The idea:** Disqualifying is part of selling. People who aren't ready get sent to free content, so the calendar only holds people who can act now.
**How he applies it:**
- **Gated calls to action:**
  - 2025 descriptions limit the call to serious founders, at a multi-thousand-dollar investment.
  - In Jan 2026: book only if you can invest, otherwise keep watching the free videos until you're ready.
- **A budget quiz on Calendly.** People who don't qualify get long-form content and are told to come back later.
- **Anti-ICP on the 2026 site:**
  - under $50k a month
  - price shoppers
  - people who want a following more than a reputation
  - people expecting results in 30 days
  - people who just want someone to post for them
- **Committed buyers only.** In the Dialed build, they only give their time to action-takers who have already shown commitment by investing, and they avoid short-term quick-money operators.
- **Wealth tiers.** High six figures up to multiple nine figures a year, from a business, a portfolio or a family office (Gstaad, Sep 2026).
- **Losing deals is acceptable.** The site says the strategist will tell you directly if it isn't a fit, and the studio would rather lose a deal than build the wrong brand.
**Where he said it:** How i built a 130k personal brand (H68gyWOlIy4, 10 Aug 2025). $52k in 3.5 months (qYIKwqRvIyU, 29 Sep 2025). $130k in 6 months (12 Jan 2026). High Ticket Offer LIVE (21 Mar 2026). Gstaad (Ze4JOAAvAq8, 12 Sep 2026). Meteoric Boost landing page (2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** High on the principle. The numbers drift when he ad-libs: the Jan 2026 video gives $3k to $5k, multiple five figures, and $300 to $5k at different points. Treat the current offer page as the source of truth for any threshold.

### 6. A high price is a filter (the virtuous pricing cycle)
**The idea:** A high price does more than earn revenue. It selects committed clients. Committed clients get better results, results attract more good clients, and the cycle repeats.
**How he applies it:**
- He prices high from the start and turns price shoppers away.
- He moved up market through trust, not volume: fewer, bigger clients won on credibility.
- He positions the brand as a status good, like a Rolex or quiet luxury: clients buy reliability plus something to show their peers, and then refer their networks (Oktoberfest).
**Where he said it:** Breaking down businesses (9 Oct 2025). The Sanskar Show #226 (Mar 2026): a high price filters for better clients. Oktoberfest (about 18 Oct 2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** High on direction. Positioning climbed from serious founders at four figures (2025) to millionaires, the top 0.1%, and a $50k-a-month floor (2026). Actual prices are not public.

### 7. The scripted close
**The idea:** The closing call follows a fixed order that gets the prospect to commit emotionally before the price comes up.
**How he applies it:** The order from his Jan 2026 breakdown:
1. Rapport.
2. Agenda.
3. Confirm they watched the pre-sale video, and take questions.
4. Emotional pain questions: why they aren't there yet, what is stopping them, how long it has been, when they realized it.
5. Pre-handle objections: why now, the cost of doing nothing, what they've tried, whether he is the right person, whether they've invested in themselves before, whether they decide alone.
6. Paint the dream state.
7. Recap their pains, goals and motivation.
8. Ask what he missed.
9. Ask for a 0 to 10 readiness rating, and probe until it reaches 10.
10. State the price casually, alongside what's included.
11. Handle objections with stories, quotes and metaphors.
12. Take payment on the call (preferred), or a deposit plus a booked follow-up.

His underlying check (The Sanskar Show): the prospect feels the pain, pictures the dream outcome, sees the gap, and sees Moksh as the credible way across it.
**Where he said it:** $130k in 6 months (12 Jan 2026). The Sanskar Show #226 (Mar 2026). High Ticket Offer LIVE (Mar 2026), which calls it a one-call close.
**Evolution / confidence:** High. He learned this the hard way:
- For roughly his first six weeks he couldn't close high-ticket deals, and he calls it a skill gap.
- He hired a sales coach, sent him Fathom call recordings, and says his close rate rose sharply. He gives no figures (Breaking down businesses, Oct 2025; The Sanskar Show).
- Real selling started in July 2025.

The typical deal size he gave in Jan 2026 is $5k to $6k (self-reported).

### 8. Objections: find the real one, hold your frame
**The idea:** The first objection is rarely the real one. Find the real one, deal with it, and don't lose composure or leave the call without a decision.
**How he applies it:**
- **"Let me think about it" is a mask.** It usually means no money, no belief in him, or a competitor. Find the real objection, dig deeper, stack several yeses, then ask again. He described getting three yeses from a prospect who first asked for time to think.
- **Reframe a delay as part of the process.** On his toughest call, a Hong Kong SaaS founder wanted to start in two weeks, after travelling. Moksh answered that those two weeks are when the foundations, team and first content batch get built, so content would be ready when he landed.
- **Point at the pain when they want to do it themselves.** The same founder had spent two years trying, with expensive gear and a lit studio, and got no traction.
- **Appeal to identity.** He described a founder who had already become a thought leader.
- **Flex on terms, not price.** He offered a deposit upfront and invoicing later.
- **Hold frame, connect emotionally, don't leave until it closes.** That call ran about two hours, and the founder signed.
- **Closer training (Dialed):**
  - Handle objections before they come up.
  - Use trap questions.
  - Read whether a prospect will act or procrastinate.
  - English builds authority on a call, Hindi builds connection. This was said about closers, not set as a content rule.
**Where he said it:** The Sanskar Show #226, toughest sales call chapter (Mar 2026). $130k in 6 months (Jan 2026). High Ticket Offer LIVE (Mar 2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** High for The Sanskar Show material. The closer curriculum may be partly Arjun's, because the transcript has no speaker labels.

### 9. The pre-sale video does the convincing
**The idea:** A recorded asset moves the prospect from researching to ready to act, so the closing call confirms a decision instead of making one.
**How he applies it:**
- **What he sends:**
  - an unlisted video (Loom) showing process and outcomes, 48 to 72 hours after the qualifying call (Jan 2026)
  - a 20 to 30 minute pre-sell video, for founders with small audiences (The Sanskar Show)
  - a long video in DMs as a nurture asset
- **Long-form doubles as pre-selling.** His own long-form YouTube videos do this job, and his bio links to one.
- **Presold inbound.** While his roaming SIM was dead in Monaco, his team closed a client worth about $6k. That client, an AI-infrastructure founder at $70k+ MRR, arrived presold after watching part of a podcast with another client that had only a few thousand views. He was onboarded within 48 hours (self-reported).
- **The two-day research window.** Prospects check YouTube, Instagram, company pages and LinkedIn before reaching out, so every surface has to convert. A founder told him he wouldn't have signed without the Monaco content (Oktoberfest).
**Where he said it:** $130k in 6 months (Jan 2026). The Sanskar Show #226 (Mar 2026). Build a brand from scratch LIVE (about Nov 2025). Monaco (M8TkhDOCZlE, about Sep 2026). Oktoberfest (about 18 Oct 2026). Instagram profile breakdown (about Sep 2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** High. It moved from a single Loom sent after a call (2025) to a whole library of public long-form content that presells before any contact (2026).

### 10. The qualifying call is short, has no price, and builds authority
**The idea:** The first call checks fit and sets authority. It does not sell.
**How he applies it:**
- About 15 minutes. He warns against going longer.
- No price talk.
- Qualify on budget, authority, need and timing (BANT).
- By Jan 2026, a head of sales and appointment setting runs DMs, setting and discovery calls.
- Show-up rate improved with Calendly and WhatsApp reminders.
**Where he said it:** $130k in 6 months (Jan 2026). The Sanskar Show #226 (Mar 2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** Medium. The 2026 booking link ("Meteoric 1:1" with Arjun Sharin) is a 60-minute call that assesses goals, commitment and the current situation. That doesn't match the 15-minute rule. It may be a combined fit-and-close call. Ask Moksh before scripting a call length.

### 11. Clients buy a new identity (the "2.0" self), not deliverables
**The idea:** People don't pay for posts or edits. They pay to become a different version of themselves, and the brand follows from that new lifestyle.
**How he applies it:**
- **The fitness-trainer analogy.** A weak trainer hands over a meal plan and a workout schedule. A great one builds a lifestyle and an identity. Meteoric turns successful founders into a "2.0" version of themselves (Feb 2026).
- **Sell the transformation from point A to point B** (Jan 2026).
- **Target what the client hasn't solved.** One 22-year-old client had money, network and a business but no social status, and Moksh sold him exactly that missing piece (case study, 30 Mar 2026).
- **Build the operator's lifestyle into the product:**
  - In Dialed, fitness, travel, meditation and emotional resilience were the closers' edge.
  - In the fitness build, status vocabulary and gear (wearables, HRV tracking) were chosen to attract affluent buyers.
**Where he said it:** Road to $1 million in 2026 (9SBwERbSze4, 21 Feb 2026). $130k in 6 months (Jan 2026). 22 year old millionaire case study (sD4R8Z4H6U4, 30 Mar 2026). Oktoberfest (Oct 2026). High Ticket Offer LIVE (Mar 2026). Build a brand from scratch LIVE (about Nov 2025).
**Evolution / confidence:** High. In 2025 his language was about monetizing a personal brand. In 2026 it shifts to status, recognition and identity.

### 12. Done-with-you vs done-for-you (a real shift)
**The idea:** In 2025 to early 2026 he argued that done-with-you coaching beats a done-for-you agency. By mid-2026 the flagship is sold as done-for-you. This is a shift in delivery, not a contradiction to hide.
**How he applies it (chronology):**
- **Sep 2025 workshop.** Both models are listed as high-ticket routes. Courses and templates are the low-ticket route.
- **Jan 2026.** Done-with-you sells a transformation from A to B, while agencies get paid per deliverable. Done-with-you wins on margin and leverage and avoids the fulfillment treadmill. He warns against the agency deliverable model.
- **Feb 2026.** The typical Indian path is freelancer, then done-for-you agency. Few people build a true info-coaching company, which in his terms means implementing systems *with* clients, not *for* them, and not just selling a course.
- **2026 studio site.** A 3-month done-for-you partnership: "Not another content agency", no homework, and the founder never touches anything the team can do. The founder gives 4 to 8 hours a month on camera.
- **Dialed (Mar 2026).** Done-for-you on the B2B side, a cohort program on the B2C side.
**Where he said it:** $52k in 3.5 months (Sep 2025). $130k in 6 months (Jan 2026). Road to $1 million (Feb 2026). High Ticket Offer LIVE (Mar 2026). Landing page (2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** High that both positions were stated. He hasn't explained the change. A reasonable reading (inference): the team now runs production, but the positioning still rejects "agency" thinking, because it sells a system and an outcome, not a list of deliverables. When writing for him today, use the current site language. When explaining his history, keep the done-with-you argument.

### 14. Organic before ads
**The idea:** Build an organic system that converts before paying for traffic. Ads amplify a working system; they don't fix a broken one.
**How he applies it:**
- **No ads into 2026.** His Jan 2026 title says "(without ads)".
- **Near-zero CAC (self-reported).** About $50 to $100 per client in Jan 2026. Effectively zero and all inbound in Oct 2025.
- **Ads come later**, as an amplifier.
- **A short-term vs long-term split.** In Monaco: ads, cold outreach and intros are short-term tools, and a founder-led brand is the long-term 10x lever.
- **By about Sep 2026 he says he is learning paid media** as a new channel.
**Where he said it:** $130k in 6 months (12 Jan 2026). Breaking down businesses (Oct 2025). Monaco (about Sep 2026). Lessons working with the top 0.1% (xQUlUeG46hs, about Sep 2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** High, with a clear sequence: organic only in 2025, paid media being added in 2026. The site's Meta pixel fits that (an inference).

### 15. Founders should not close their own calls
**The idea:** Once demand exists, a founder taking sales calls is a bad use of the scarcest resource and weakens their authority.
**How he applies it:**
- **The reasons, from Dialed:**
  - Founders don't have time to take every call.
  - Closing lowers the founder's authority.
  - Founders are usually weaker closers than specialists.
  - Every hour spent closing is an hour taken from content and lead generation.
- **Closers need warm inbound,** so lead generation drives the whole system.
- **His own path:**
  - In 2025 he closed his own deals while learning sales.
  - By Feb 2026, closers keep closing while leadership builds new offers.
  - Booking links moved from his own Calendly to a team calendar under Arjun Sharin's name.
**Where he said it:** High Ticket Offer LIVE (Mar 2026). Road to $1 million (Feb 2026). $130k in 6 months (Jan 2026). Video descriptions 2025 to 2026.
**Evolution / confidence:** High.

### 16. The first month decides retention
**The idea:** Buyer's remorse is killed by an early win, so the start of an engagement is engineered for momentum.
**How he applies it:**
- Get a quick win in month one: the first cash collected or the first deal closed.
- Stay hands-on and easy to reach early, including quick WhatsApp calls.
- Check in 3 to 4 times a week, plus weekly calls (Dialed B2B).
- Front-load momentum by delivering a roadmap within 24 hours. In the fitness build he calls this front-loading dopamine.
- He says Meteoric cut the time to a client's first post and first win from about 21 days to about 12 (self-reported).
- On the site, client content goes live from about week 3.
**Where he said it:** High Ticket Offer LIVE (Mar 2026), partly from Arjun's account of being onboarded by Meteoric. Build a brand from scratch LIVE (about Nov 2025). Breaking down businesses (Oct 2025). Landing page (2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** High.

### 17. Sell the feeling and the pain, not the information
**The idea:** People pay to stop feeling stuck. The seller's job is to help the prospect get out of their own way, which means not avoiding confrontation.
**How he applies it:**
- Emotional pain questions and a dream state come before the price (model 7).
- On sales calls, being "ruthless" means pressing on pain points and quoting high prices, decisively but not cruelly (Dec 2025).
- In the Dialed session they agree that a closer should call out procrastination instead of pushing a sale.
**Where he said it:** High Ticket Offer LIVE (Mar 2026). $130k in 6 months (Jan 2026). How i made 2025 my best year yet (nnMuox4BtbU, 30 Dec 2025).
**Evolution / confidence:** Medium to high. A line from the Dialed session (closing the sale buys you dinner, delivering results funds your retirement) can't be attributed to a speaker, so don't quote it as his.

### 18. Accountability both ways instead of a guarantee
**The idea:** No refund or performance guarantee. Instead, both sides commit to their part, and the agency holds itself to a higher bar than the client's goal.
**How he applies it:**
- **Dialed:**
  - The agency sets its internal benchmark above the client's goal ($15k against the owner's $10k).
  - It owns any shortfall if the leads were good.
  - The owner must supply good leads.
- **The headline promise carries the condition that makes it true** (model 2).
- **The 2026 site:**
  - No guarantee of followers or revenue.
  - If the founder does their part and the plan's goals aren't met, the studio keeps working at no cost.
**Where he said it:** High Ticket Offer LIVE (Mar 2026). Landing page (2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** High. The real scope sits in a private contract the site refers to.

### 19. You don't need reach to sell (money from 1,000 views)
**The idea:** For a high-ticket service, a small audience of the right people plus a trust system can close deals. Views matter only for reach content.
**How he applies it:**
- **The small-account playbook (The Sanskar Show):**
  - a short list of high-value followers
  - trust content
  - an optimized profile
  - a 20 to 30 minute pre-sell video
  - an appointment setter sending 50 to 100 targeted DMs a day
- **Every step is tracked:** open rate, reply rate, DM-to-conversation rate, calls booked, show-up rate and close rate.
- **His example** is a client running an offshore dev-team consultancy for US oil and gas companies.
- **Silent buyers.** A podcast with a few thousand views closed a client because the right 10 to 15 people saw it (Monaco). His own buyers almost never liked or commented before signing (Gstaad).
**Where he said it:** The Sanskar Show #226, money from 1,000 views chapter (Mar 2026). Monaco (about Sep 2026). Gstaad (12 Sep 2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** High. It overlaps with the views-vs-revenue models in [personal-brand.md](personal-brand.md).

### 20. Luxury is the feeling of being taken care of
**The idea:** A premium price is justified by the whole experience, not only the output. His definition: "luxury is as simple as the feeling of being taken care of" (Oktoberfest).
**How he applies it:**
- **Examples he gives:** business-class flights, a custom-engraved perfume bottle, cashmere blankets at a client dinner in Stuttgart, a relationship with a Rolex store.
- **Meteoric aims for that feeling across the whole engagement,** from the first WhatsApp message to the final testimonial shoot. That includes flying the team to clients' cities.
- **In-person client retreats are part of delivery.** The Bali retreat video description (May 2026) says serving clients well and building real relationships come first.
**Where he said it:** Oktoberfest (about 18 Oct 2026). "don't build a personal brand in 2026" description (qSNYWCyKGyo, 30 May 2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** Medium. It is recent and comes from one spoken source plus a description, but it is consistent with the high-touch positioning since 2025.

### 21. Cap capacity and ration access
**The idea:** Limited spots keep delivery honest and create real scarcity.
**How he applies it:**
- In Dialed, about 10 closers per intake, so they don't overpromise. The B2B fee rises as demand grows.
- The fitness story sequence offers two slots.
- Ration your calls to action. He cites a peer, Saul, offering 15 free discovery calls as an example he endorses.
**Where he said it:** High Ticket Offer LIVE (Mar 2026). Build a brand from scratch LIVE (about Nov 2025). $52k in 3.5 months workshop (Sep 2025).
**Evolution / confidence:** Medium. He prescribes it for clients' offers. He has not stated a capacity cap for Meteoric itself.

### 22. Land, deliver, then upsell for lifetime value
**The idea:** The first program is the entry point. A strong client experience earns a longer, higher-priced second program.
**How he applies it:**
- A three-month program, followed by an upsell to a six-month, higher-priced program to raise lifetime value (Oct 2025).
- The model stays sustainable as long as prices rise with results and the client experience drives renewals.
- In the funnel, the upsell comes after the quick win, the testimonial and delivery.
**Where he said it:** Breaking down businesses (Oct 2025). The $100 and $10,000 figures in that episode were illustrations, not prices. High Ticket Offer LIVE (Mar 2026).
**Evolution / confidence:** Medium. One clear statement each. The 2026 site describes only the 3-month partnership.
