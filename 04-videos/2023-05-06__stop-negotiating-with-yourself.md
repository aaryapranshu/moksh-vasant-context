# stop negotiating with yourself.

A mid-semester reset video: when exams pile up and his routine slips, Moksh cuts back to a short list of daily non-negotiables and three hours of deep work, then takes the same energy into a university cricket tournament.

## Core thesis
When life gets busy, do not renegotiate every habit every day. Fix a small set of non-negotiables, protect them, and let everything else flex. Pair that with deep, distraction-free work, which feels uncomfortable at first. His closing advice: go to the extreme when trying something new, then pull back to what is sustainable, and do not worry about being judged for it.

## Mental models and frameworks
- **Non-negotiables.** A short list of daily habits that are never up for debate. Removes daily decision-making about the basics. (Borrowed from Iman Gadzhi, credited on camera.)
- **Deep work vs shallow work** (*Deep Work*; the author is not named in the video). Protect blocks of focus; expect the first stretch to be uncomfortable.
- **Extreme, then sustainable.** Overshoot when experimenting, then settle at a level you can keep.
- **Life as a public project.** Documenting self-experiments on YouTube. The description adds that he is not a self-help guru, just treating himself as a project to improve, and thanks viewers for following his growth.
- **Imperfection disclosed.** He names his own slip before giving advice. This builds trust without claiming authority.
- **Rooms of more experienced people.** Joining a consulting society and attending industry events. (Connects to the bamboo video's idea of using inferiority as fuel.)
