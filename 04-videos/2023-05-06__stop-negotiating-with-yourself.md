# stop negotiating with yourself.

A mid-semester reset video: when exams pile up and his routine slips, Moksh cuts back to six daily non-negotiables and three hours of deep work, then takes the same energy into a university Asia Cup cricket match against Pakistan.

**Caveats:** auto-captions (fairly clean in this video). The cricket section is mostly crowd noise and banter; the match result is not stated. One line of mock commentary says "Mox" injured both hamstrings [07:30]; it is played for laughs and may not be literal.

## Core thesis

"Life's all about energy. It's the most important resource you have" [01:45]. He has the most energy when he sticks to a routine of non-negotiables, so when life gets busy he stops renegotiating every habit and protects a short list, plus three hours of deep work a day, and lets the rest flex [02:11] to [03:03]. His closing advice: "go extreme with things and then tone it down" [08:37], and do not worry about being judged.

## Mental models and frameworks

- **Energy as the main resource** [01:45]. This predates his 2024 Short "as a 20 year old energy is my greatest currency" (wekKGWBM0GY).
- **Non-negotiables** [02:11]. A short daily list never up for debate, credited to Iman Gadzhi.
- **Deep work vs shallow work** [02:23] to [03:03]. Protect focus blocks; expect a stretch of discomfort before flow.
- **Extreme, then sustainable** [08:37].
- **Life as a public project** [00:17]. The description: "While I am no self help guru with a right to preach, I enjoy treating myself as a project."
- **Imperfection disclosed before advice** [00:39], [08:27].
