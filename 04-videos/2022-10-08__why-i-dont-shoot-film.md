# This is why I DONT shoot film

A slow Sunday vlog from his first months at university in Sydney, where the clickbait "reason" turns out to be gentle and personal: his mother's old film camera will not turn on.

## Core idea
The title promises a contrarian take on film photography. The payoff is a small setback: he brought back a family film camera to document his university years, bought film and a battery, and it still will not power on. The rest is a day in Newtown, a sunrise, and a cricket match with friends. He never argues against film; he clearly loves its look.

## Walkthrough
Order of content, paraphrased:

1. **Opener.** A plain greeting. It is Sunday, he slept in, and the week was hectic.
2. **The film camera.** On a recent trip to India he took his mother's high-school film camera from his dad's closet. Her grandfather had given it to her to document her school years. Moksh wants to use it the same way for his university life, because he loves how film looks (he calls it aesthetic). He bought film and a battery at a Kodak store, but the camera still will not turn on, so the plan is on hold.
3. **Newtown.** A walk along the street near his accommodation: cafes, bars, galleries, art stores, vintage thrift shops. He hangs out there every few days. He is thrilled to learn Coldplay's "A Sky Full of Stars" video was shot there, since it is one of his favorite songs.
4. **Sunrise.** A 6:15am sunrise over the skyline, and a resolution to wake up for every sunrise.
5. **Cricket.** Watching India vs Pakistan in the Asia Cup with Pakistani friends: friendly banter, and a shirt-off celebration when Hardik Pandya hit the winning six. It rekindled his love of cricket.
6. **Rest and room.** Short on sleep, resting before a busy week. He has put artwork on his walls and starts to show it, which is where the captured transcript ends.

## Mental models and creative philosophy
- **Film for its look, not as a purist.** "Aesthetic" is his word for visual taste and his highest praise.
- **Documentation as inheritance.** The same camera has passed through three generations, each recording a life stage. He frames his own filming as continuing a family habit, not as content strategy.
- **Places carry story.** A street matters more because a favorite music video was shot there. (Inference: an early sign of location-as-story thinking, which shows up later in his location-led videos and hooks.)
- **Emotional beats from light.** Sunrise and skyline shots are used as the feeling moments of the edit.
- Light life rules: wake for sunrise, rest before a hard week, enjoy shared moments across rivalries.
