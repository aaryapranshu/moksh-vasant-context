# This is why I DONT shoot film

A slow Sunday vlog from his second semester in Sydney, where the clickbait "reason" is gentle and personal: his mother's old film camera, brought back from India to document his university life, will not turn on.

**Caveats:** auto-captions, clean. Captions end at [03:44] as he starts showing his room; the rest is music. Earlier versions of this file said the camera was a gift from his mother's grandfather and that this was his "first months" at university; the transcript says "my grandfather" (Moksh's grandfather) and the timing is his second semester.

## Core thesis

The title promises a contrarian take on film photography. The payoff is a small setback: the camera does not work, so he has to wait. He never argues against film; he loves its look. The rest is a day of small pleasures: Newtown, a 6:15 a.m. sunrise, and watching India beat Pakistan with Pakistani friends.
