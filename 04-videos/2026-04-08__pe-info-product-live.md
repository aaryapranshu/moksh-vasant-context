# Watch me build a $1m private equity info product (LIVE)

Moksh sits with the founder of an acquisitions firm (Pocket Fund, rebranding to a name transcribed "Kautalia") and his senior analyst, and turns their done-for-you M&A advisory into a done-with-you consulting offer for first-time business buyers. No price is set on camera.

## Core thesis

A founder who already has an audience and real expertise does not need more reach; he needs a productized offer, clear positioning in his buyer's words, and a simple funnel that plugs the audience into it. For a sophisticated market, inbound is not the problem: "I don't think inbound lead flow will ever be an issue. It's just kind of like making that river, making that pathway for the funnels" (Moksh) [13:46]. In a done-with-you offer you charge for expertise and access, not the outcome: "the success of them actually making the acquisition is not the thing you're charging them for but what you're charging them for is your expertise your consulting, your trademarks, your frameworks" [37:46 to 38:02].

### Principles (Moksh unless noted)

- **Positioning test:** "when they think of a problem the solution is like the person" [14:37].
- **Words pick the market:** "saying one word and not the other word makes you attract a whole different side of the market" [15:29]; he uses "system" and "leverage" on purpose to attract founders [16:27].
- **Sophistication differs by platform;** don't touch a channel that already works (LinkedIn) [16:39, 35:41].
- **YouTube nurtures, it doesn't acquire** [17:08].
- **Audiences contain talent:** unqualified followers become hires [21:20, 22:29].
- **Target from the content you already made:** curiosity content built a first-time-buyer audience, so sell to first-time buyers [34:51].
- **DWY charges for expertise and access, not the result** [37:46 to 38:40].
- **Sell deliverables, not hours** (the firm's own lesson, client side) [45:43 to 46:14].
- **Solve one problem, create the next; the next is the upsell** [55:18].
- **The offer is the problem-solution set** [56:46].
- **Scale coaching by shifting the split** from 1:1 time toward pre-recorded content and frameworks as proof builds [58:09 to 59:08].
- **Pre-sale asset is credibility, then process** [1:03:45].
- **Intro is about their problems, not your story** [1:04:44 to 1:05:16].
- **Execution beats perfection** in clients [1:08:32].
- **Curation for reach, direct pain for conversion:** TOFU can be reporter-style; MOFU must make the ICP "feel hurt" [1:11:26, 1:20:25].
- **Stories move buyers to 80%, sales finishes** [1:22:11]; "marketing to do majority of the conversion whereas sales just does like 20%" [1:22:29].
- **Search fund as business school:** sourcing = lead gen, outreach = outreach, pricing a deal = sales [41:10 to 41:25].

### Comparison with the earlier live builds (same series)

Moksh himself links the series: "I did a fitness and I've done a high ticket sales" [1:09:24]. Earlier: [Peak Form Protocol with Dhanil](2025-10-21__build-brand-from-scratch-live.md) (Oct 2025) and [Dialed with Arjun Sharin](2026-03-21__high-ticket-offer-best-closer.md) (Mar 2026).

**Consistent across all three:**
- The operator's proof opens the session before any building.
- An audit of what assets exist before writing the offer.
- A named offer with a "zing" (Peak Form Protocol, Dialed, Buying a business in a box), with the Meteoric Boost Protocol as the reference [57:04].
- An ICP pass, then a stack of problems (13 in Peak Form; pains plus three before and three after in Dialed; 9 here), with solutions as the flip.
- Done-with-you as the default model.
- Profile as a landing page; story sequences for inbound; a Loom-style pre-sale asset; an appointment setter; a closing call; one long-form YouTube asset in the funnel.
- Meteoric's own team (creative director, setters) is offered as part of fulfilment.
- No spoken CTA to viewers; the description's "work with me" link is Arjun's Calendly at Meteoric Boost (as in Dialed).

**What changed in this build:**
- **Monetize an existing brand, not build one.** The client already has six traffic sources and all-inbound clients, so the work is an asset and platform audit, not a brand from scratch.
- **Four-section board** (offer, ICP, traffic and lead gen, sales process) replaces Dialed's long field-by-field sequence.
- **Platform-by-platform ICP mapping** is new: different buyer types and sophistication per channel.
- **No price.** Peak Form set $1,500 for 4 months; Dialed derived prices with live value math. Here pricing is only "a percentage of the value we're generating ... which is what we will calculate" [34:11].
- **Language as positioning** is explicit for the first time (Aryan Mahajan's "lead intelligence systems") [15:47].
- **Charge for expertise, not outcome,** and the content-coaching-frameworks split for scaling, are new statements of the DWY model.
- **Content formats by funnel stage** are more developed than in Dialed (which named technical, storytelling and call-recording videos): reporter, series, storytelling, edits, problem-solution, with stories bridging MOFU to BOFU and the 0 / 80 / 100% motivation model.
- **Warm outbound** sits beside story inbound from the start (Dialed's profile funnel also allowed warm outbound).
- **AI is now routine in fulfilment:** Claude projects per content format, Claude to review call transcripts, Claude for split-screen pages. Dialed only mentioned a team member scripting "with ... AI".
- **No fulfilment design** (no first-month win, benchmarks or retention talk as in Dialed), because the client's delivery process already exists.
- **No pessimistic timeline or three-before/three-after exercise.**

## Script anatomy

- **Title formula:** Watch me build a $[X] [niche] [asset] (LIVE). Matches "Watch me build a profitable personal brand from scratch (LIVE)". The dollar figure is aspirational and not substantiated on camera.
- **Hook:** a cold-open montage of the best lines from the session (system, positioning, "every single word ... matters", done-with-you, "junk in junk out") [00:00 to 01:04], then a casual greeting. New for the series: Dialed opened on banter and the guest's numbers.
- **Credibility move:** the guest's numbers ($4k to $1.8M) in the first four minutes, plus Moksh's own search-fund credential [04:23].
- **Structure:** proof, Moksh explains the industry for viewers, the four-section board, asset audit, platform-by-platform ICP, offer, client journey, problems, solutions, name, profile, funnel, content formats.
- **Retention devices:** teaching moments for the audience (valuation multiples, acqui-hire math, roll-ups); live reactions ("that's pretty sick"); jokes; references to other clients by name (Aryan, Manav, Arjun); board recaps after each section.
- **CTA:** none spoken. Description: "In this video, i build an extremely unique consulting offer with a founder who runs a micro private equity fund", then "work with me" (Arjun's 60-minute Calendly at Meteoric Boost), Moksh's Instagram and the testimonials account.
- **Funnel role:** product demo and client case study at once; doubles as fulfilment for the client.
