# How I graduated school

His school-leaving video from August 2021, at 17. The captions are unusable, but a vlog posted four days later says what it was: a farewell the students gave themselves. His other mid-2021 vlogs fill in the school story around it (CBSE class 12, board exams cancelled, results of about 85.7%).

**Caveats:** the transcript file is Devanagari noise mixing transliterated English ("school", "leaving certificate", "college", "feeling emotional", "extremely grateful to everyone", "journey", "subscribe"). No quote can be used. Everything about the content beyond the title and description comes from his own description in pLB7-S_IgLA, and is still thin. A human watch is still needed for detail.

## Core thesis

Low confidence: an emotional, music-led storytelling film about leaving school, ending on gratitude. Four days later he said: "the last two videos that I've made have been all about farewells. One video was about us students giving ourselves a farewell" (pLB7-S_IgLA [03:18]). So the "graduation" here is a student-organized farewell, not a formal ceremony; the board exams that would normally end school had been cancelled (W9NW_R6__HM [00:36] to [02:25]).
