# How I graduated school

His school graduation video from August 2021, at about 17. The auto-captions are unusable, so almost everything here comes from the title, description and music credits. Needs a human watch.

## Core idea
Low confidence: an emotional, storytelling-style look back at finishing school, ending on gratitude to the people who supported him (fragments suggest a teacher or mentor, friends and family). The packaging says more than the speech: he tagged a personal milestone #Storytelling and scored it with three credited indie and acoustic tracks.

Not reconstructable. YouTube tagged the captions as Hindi and output them in Devanagari, mixing transliterated English words with noise. Surviving fragments, in rough order, all low confidence:

- References to school life, friends, a leaving certificate, photos, college, and feeling emotional about leaving.

## Mental models and creative philosophy
- **Personal milestones as narrative.** By mid-2021 he already framed his own life events as stories (#Storytelling), not as updates.
- **Soft, emotive music taste.** Credited tracks: "Moment (Instrumental)" by Joey Howard, "False Art" by Lizzy McAlpine and Ben Kessler, "Bound To Be Something Good" by The Crystal Casino Band. Same warm acoustic and indie palette as his December 2021 farmhouse vlog.
- **Gratitude as the close.** The clearest recoverable theme. Gratitude stays a constant in his later work (his 2025 year recap lists it as an operating principle).

## Life details revealed
- Finished school in 2021, at about 17. School name and country not recoverable.

## Style and script notes
- **Title:** plain sentence case, "How I [life event]". A story promise, not a tutorial, with no number, hook device or parenthetical. Early ancestor of his 2025 to 2026 "How i..." proof titles, which keep the same opener but add money, timeframes and a lowercase "i".
- **CTA:** explicit and early. Asks for likes, shares and subscribes in the speech and again in the description ("Consider subscribing !"). His later videos drop the pleading and lead with proof instead.
- **Packaging:** premiere release, hashtags (#School #Graduation #Storytelling) and full music credits, a hobbyist filmmaker convention.
- **Voice:** earnest, sentimental, grateful (as far as can be read). No quote used; no clean line is recoverable.
