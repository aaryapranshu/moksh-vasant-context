# lessons i've learnt working with the top 0.1% (unfiltered)
Walking through Paddington in London, Moksh lists 12 mindset and relationship lessons from working with high-net-worth clients, framed as a record of how he thinks at 22.

## Core thesis
At his stage the edge comes from mental models (how you treat people, how you see risk, who you are becoming), not from tactics. He admits the video is light on tactics on purpose.

## Walkthrough

### Frame (opening)
- He says he is documenting his thinking at 22 so he can look back and compare later.

### The 12 lessons (body, in the order the notes record)
1. **Manage perception on purpose.** His clients are older than him, so he shapes how he comes across deliberately.
2. **A service business is a relationship business.** The two questions he asks: what value can I add, and what actually motivates this person?
3. **Prefer inbound networking.** He tries to be someone people want to reach out to, and he acts as a connector between people.
4. **Share wild ideas instead of small talk.** Big or unusual ideas get to a real conversation faster.
5. **Borrow from unrelated industries.** Retention from legacy businesses, pre-selling from info-product sellers, investing from wealthy clients.
6. **Paranoia is an asset.** It costs him rest but keeps him sharp.
7. **Stillness and intention over linear productivity.** He does a 15-minute visualization meditation every day (self-reported).
8. **Reference points and map expansions.** Travel and high-skill abilities like closing reset what feels possible.
9. **Learn new channels.** He is currently learning paid media.
10. **Use offers as education.** He declined an offer to acquire 50% of the company but used the process to learn how valuation works. It got him thinking about being exit-ready within two years (self-reported).
11. **Delegate low-leverage work.** He hired an executive assistant and gave them a written playbook.
12. **Credit the people behind you.** He credits his partner and his mother, and frames his drive at work as the masculine counterpart to their support.

### Close (closing)
- He admits the video was light on tactics.
- Metaphor: there is a wall between you and the pot of gold, and the wall is your own mindset and discipline (quoted below).
- He signs off with a self-deprecating line.
