# Video index

Breakdowns of 32 of Moksh's YouTube videos, plus three syntheses (Shorts, early vlogs, the creator era). Every breakdown is built from a full timestamped transcript in [../transcripts/](../transcripts/INDEX.md): YouTube auto-captions where they exist, or a local Whisper transcription from the audio where YouTube has none (marked "Whisper" below). Views are as of 20 Oct 2026. Dates are the publish dates from the transcript metadata. A few filenames are one day earlier than the publish date (a time-zone offset in the first research pass); the filenames are kept so links don't break.

Each breakdown has: core thesis, timestamped walkthrough, mental models, stories and numbers, script anatomy, voice notes.

**Syntheses**
- [shorts-2024-2025.md](shorts-2024-2025.md): all 49 uploads of 3 minutes or less. The core is 43 Shorts (Sep 2024 to Jun 2025), 32 with a spoken script. They look like re-posts of his Instagram reels, so this is the closest thing in the repo to his reel scripts.
- [early-vlogs-2020-2022.md](early-vlogs-2020-2022.md): index of all 75 creator-era uploads with a transcript, plus school, Jamnagar, the first Meteoric (2021), the move to Sydney.
- [early-era-synthesis.md](early-era-synthesis.md): what the 2021 to 2023 creator years explain about how he builds brands now.

**Client testimonials** from the Meteoric Boost channel are in [../02-meteoric-boost/testimonials/](../02-meteoric-boost/testimonials/README.md).
