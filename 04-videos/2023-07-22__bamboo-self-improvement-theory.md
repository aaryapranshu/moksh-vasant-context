# this self improvement theory transformed my life.

A reflective short film that organizes a whole semester around one image, bamboo growth: the underground phase was his December 2022 to February 2023 break at home in India, the emergence was flying back to Sydney, and the visible growth was the semester that followed (new societies, facing maths, new friends). It is his most viewed pre-2025 long-form video (20,401 views).

**Caveats:** auto-captions, so a few words are garbled (one section of group chatter at [02:13] to [02:41] is unreadable). Two conversation scenes feature a friend who is on exchange from NYU; the captions do not label speakers, so attribution in those scenes is inferred from context. The description is quoted where it adds meaning.

## Core thesis

Visible success is the last and smallest part of growth. A bamboo shoots up 90 feet in six weeks, but that is "just 20[%] of the process" [00:13]; the roots take three to five years [00:26]. He applies the same order to his own life: lay the foundation, build self-belief, then set long, medium and short-term goals and chase them [00:39]. The semester was the proof: the foundation he built at home let him grab opportunities in Sydney. Book learning alone is not enough; it has to be combined with real-world reps.

## Walkthrough

1. **[00:00] The bamboo hook.** "I love bamboos, particularly the way they grow." Come back to a small bamboo six weeks later and it is "thrice your size". "That's like an overnight success, isn't it?" [00:13]. The 90-foot growth is about 20% of the process; the root system takes three to five years [00:26].
2. **[00:39] The order of operations.** Foundation first, confidence in yourself, then long, medium and short-term goals. Bamboo growth "has been a central theme for my third semester of university" [00:39].
3. **[00:53] Underground phase.** Going back home, reconnecting with family, the gym five to six times a week, ice baths every day, and "about 10 to 11 self-improvement books". (This was the December 2022 to February 2023 break at home in India. He describes the same weeks in "this is how memories are made." [01:38]: from 6 December to Christmas he did "self-improvement, doing all sorts of uncomfortable things", gym, daily reading and ice baths.)
4. **[01:05] Emergence.** The February flight from India to Sydney "marked my emergence from the ground". He titled his first video back "I'm putting myself out there" because "if you don't shoot the shot it's a definite miss" [01:18].
5. **[01:32] New rooms.** He joined 180 Degrees Consulting, "a not-for-profit business consulting society", and a real estate investment student association "as their creative director" [01:45]. Around people with more life experience, he had two options: shy away from the fact "that I'm not as good as I think I am", or "use that feeling of inferiority to push me to grow" [01:59]. He chose the second: observe, learn "through osmosis", "like an internship almost", learning "in a very multi-dimensional and holistic way" [02:13].
6. **[02:41] Facing maths.** He feared maths since failing an exam in ninth grade, "this paralyzing fear of math" [02:56]. In first year he picked units without much maths [03:09]; this semester three of his four units were numbers-heavy [03:21]. "Math and economics is all concept based" [03:33]. He did "much better than I thought I would" and expects his degree to demand more maths, which he welcomes because he will end up with "a much better analytical mind" [03:47].
7. **[04:19] The exchange friend.** A friend introduces himself: he came to Sydney on an exchange between NYU and the University of Sydney and is on his second-to-last day. They met sharing the chest-fly machine at the gym [04:46] and went rock climbing together. The downside of exchange friends is that they leave [04:59]; "exchange goes both ways", so Moksh may end up in New York one day [05:13].
8. **[05:55] Interstellar and rounded knowledge.** Interstellar works because it "combines science and art" [05:55]. He calls this "rounded knowledge", from Robert Greene's *Mastery* [06:23]. "I'm pretty good with art. I know how to invoke emotions in people through my filmmaking" because "I am an emotional person" [06:36]. "What I'm now working on is my analytical side" [06:50]. That is "the road to mastery" [07:04].
9. **[07:04] Leaving the room.** Someone asks the friend whether he is attached to the room he is leaving. The reply, most likely from the departing exchange friend (speakers are not labelled): "part of the journey is the end", and if he cries it will be for leaving the people, not the room [07:17] to [07:30]. (Earlier versions of this file attributed this to Moksh; the context points to the friend.)
10. **[07:43] Verdict.** "This semester has been my best semester at university so far and probably one of the most life-changing periods", because "I treated it as an internship or an apprentice[ship]". As a kid he believed he could learn everything from self-improvement books at home, but "self-education doesn't amount to anything if it's not combined with real life experiences" [07:57]. No point dwelling on what he could have done better [08:09]. "I have to trust the process and trust the bamboo growth" [08:22].
11. **[08:36] Close.** "This is just me reflecting on my life as I always do on this channel." Credits *Mastery* as his main inspiration and says he is filming a review of it [08:49]. The last shot: the "light at the end of the tunnel" through exam season was "a cup of homemade chai" [09:04] to [09:17].

## Mental models and frameworks

- **Bamboo growth (invisible compounding before visible results).** He first relayed this as an *Atomic Habits* illustration in March 2023 (V6RXQ1_T_DA [03:41], where he gives the root phase as five to six years); here it becomes his own organizing idea, with three to five years [00:26]. It also named his Instagram "Bamboo Grow" reels (description: "I make reels documenting my 'Bamboo growth' as a student/athlete/content creator") and the first version of his Notion template ("bamboo brain", per EP.52). The description adds: "Once the foundation is established, the growth is meteoric."
- **Foundation, confidence, then tiered goals** [00:39].
- **Inferiority as fuel** [01:59]. Get into rooms of people better than you and let the gap push you.
- **Learning by osmosis, apprenticeship over self-study** [02:13], [07:57].
- **Run toward the fear** [02:56] to [03:47]. He picked the units he had avoided.
- **Rounded knowledge: art plus science** [06:23] to [07:04]. He names emotion as his edge and analysis as his deliberate growth area. (Inference: this explains the later pairing of creative direction with revenue proof at Meteoric Boost.)
- **Metaphor as the spine of a story.** One natural image frames months of footage.

## Script anatomy

- **Title:** all lowercase, ending in a period, a curiosity gap ("this ... theory" without naming it). Tagged #selfimprovement #bamboogrowth #shortfilm. He positions it as a film.
- **Hook:** a personal statement of love for an object ("I love bamboos") followed by a puzzle ("how can a bamboo shoot up 90 feet into the air within six weeks?") [00:00] to [00:13].
- **Structure:** metaphor, the order of operations, underground phase, emergence, three proof stories (societies, maths, a friendship), a borrowed framework (rounded knowledge), verdict, close. Each story is tied back to the metaphor ("everything relates back to one central theme and that's bamboo growth" [03:59]).
- **Cross-promotion:** names his previous video by title inside the script [01:18].
- **Close:** reflection ("as I always do on this channel"), source credit, a soft personal moment (chai). No subscribe ask. The description links Instagram and his newsletter (beacons.ai).
- **Performance note:** 20,401 views, the most of any pre-2025 long-form video. The next best are "stop negotiating with yourself." (17,425) and "you need to start looking." (11,853).
