# you need to start looking.

A short film on awareness: inspired by Batman's eye for detail and his father's repeated advice to stay present, Moksh drops music on walks, stops multitasking, and practises noticing people and surroundings.

## Core thesis
Awareness is a skill and the first step to everything else. Most people are too busy doing to notice anything. Being present (no phone, no music, no multitasking, watching details and body language) lets you read people and situations, and it is a practice you keep returning to, not a state you reach. The description frames the film as mindfulness in a fast-moving world and says he is documenting this while far from perfect.

## Mental models and frameworks
- **Awareness first.** Noticing is the first step before any action; he treats it as the most important skill to train.
- **Being over doing.** Constant activity crowds out attention.
- **Presence through subtraction.** Remove inputs (phone, music, multitasking) rather than add techniques.
- **Read people.** Body language and small details as information. (Inference: this is the observational base for his later sales and client-extraction work, where reading a founder is the job.)
- **Flow through physical focus.** A fast hike forces single-point attention.
- **Practice, not perfection.** Admits frequent distraction; the skill is returning.
- **Lineage.** Continues the Osho watchfulness practice he described in Jan 2023 (watching his walking, thoughts and surroundings), here framed through his father's advice. Later echoed by the zero-judgment observation he takes from *The Inner Game of Tennis* (Wiser Together EP.60, Feb 2026).
