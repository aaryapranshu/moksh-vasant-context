# you need to start looking.

A short film on awareness: inspired by Batman's eye for detail and his father's repeated advice to stay present, Moksh drops music on walks, stops multitasking and practises noticing people and surroundings. Built around a hike in Royal National Park with his friend Sean and coffee with the creator Dihann.

**Caveats:** auto-captions; long stretches are music only ([03:22] to [04:19], [06:03] to [07:34]), so the conversation with Dihann is mostly not captioned. Speakers are not labelled. The cold open and the post-hike reflection on flow are the same exchange, used twice, and are spoken mostly by his hiking companion, not by Moksh.

## Core thesis

Awareness is the first skill. "Human beings, at the end of the day, we're not human doings" [00:49], [10:32]. Being present (no phone, no music, no multitasking, watching details and body language) lets you read people and situations. It is a practice you return to after slipping, not a state you reach. The description frames it as "the power of being mindful in a world that moves so fast" and adds "I'm just documenting my experiences and learnings along the way, and I'm far from perfect."

## Mental models and frameworks

- **Being over doing** ("we're not human doings") [00:49], [10:32].
- **Awareness first** [01:33]: noticing precedes any action.
- **Presence through subtraction** [12:09]: remove inputs (phone, music, multitasking).
- **Read people** [01:21], [12:09]: body language and small details as information. (Inference: the observational base for his later sales and extraction work with founders.)
- **Flow from single-point physical focus** [00:17], [11:30].
- **Practice, not perfection** [12:33].
- **Lineage:** continues the Osho watchfulness practice he described in Jan 2023 (-MGRRBmou1U [07:47] to [08:43]), here framed through his father.
