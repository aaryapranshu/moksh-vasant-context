## Summary

- **Dates and cadence.** The era opens with a bulk upload of 12 Shorts on 27 Sep 2024, then 5 in Oct 2024, 3 in Nov 2024, none in Dec, 4 in Jan 2025, 5 in Feb, 2 in Mar, 5 in Apr (3 on 12 Apr), none in May, and a daily run of 7 from 4 to 10 Jun 2025. Then Shorts stop. The next upload is the long-form "How i built a 130k personal brand" on 10 Aug 2025.
- **These are almost certainly Instagram reels re-posted to YouTube** (inference). The 27 Sep 2024 batch includes a Father's Day reel ("So happy Father's Day, Dad", 0MPbEYQDK7Q [00:28]), which points to a reel first made around June. p6Qg2wproJo opens "as a 20-year-old Clarity of thought is my superpower" [00:00], the same hook he later says made his breakout reel (The Sanskar Show #226). On YouTube it has 6,416 views, so the YouTube copies are not where the reach happened.
- **Spoken vs music-only.** 32 of the 43 have a spoken voiceover script. 11 are music-only edits (lyrics only in the transcript): HgN5LXYdvRs, OUJzbWgxw38, EsxKG67VYA4, HosTrfbY5h0, WVGhbbHwgZg, loIrqvMTQ3s, Rg0qztVmVQo, AchyPjy6viY, Xe8TA6v1nXs, f-Ymh_ZyFvQ, ITJOKAwuz9g. Their titles carry the idea; any on-screen text is not captured. Do not quote their lyrics as his words.
- **Themes.** Running his life like a company (systems, leverage, compounding, ROI, MVPs, a "board meeting with myself", "maximize stakeholder value"); the student-founder identity (economics at the University of Sydney, the private equity / search fund internship, StuKonnect); peak performance routines (deep-work sprints, meditation, hybrid athlete); social intelligence and seeing "one layer deeper"; life as a story he narrates.
- **How it relates to the 2025 pivot.** No Short mentions Meteoric Boost, clients or a personal brand offer. But the bridge is visible: the business vocabulary applied to himself (cap table, valuations, stakeholder value) becomes the founder vocabulary of Meteoric Boost; the comment-keyword CTA ("comment the word perpetual / epiphanies / 2025") is the precursor of "DM METEORIC"; and the final Short (10 Jun 2025, two days after his first client per the timeline) names the two domains he chose for the year: "Online marketing to hold, move, and convert attention at scale and the world of finance, macroeconomics, and deals" (7KzjZKryW6k [00:00]). After that he stops posting Shorts on YouTube and returns as a founder with long-form.

## Script structure of a typical Short

- **Length:** 25 to 62 seconds for spoken Shorts (most 37 to 59), apart from three one-line aphorism Shorts of 12 to 25 seconds. A full script runs about 145 to 260 words, roughly 4 words a second: a fast, continuous voiceover over B-roll with no pauses.
- **Beats:**
  1. **Hook (1 line):** age plus identity, or a thesis line.
  2. **Turn or thesis (1 line):** "And here's what I've realized", "Let me explain", "But, the real flex is", "I've come to the realization that".
  3. **Body (3 to 5 points):** concrete personal practices, often numbered ("Number one, maniac alert", p7ZODv8gmiA; "step one", xJ8soSnkuRY), using precise specifics (45-minute sprints, 4 hours of output, maker's and manager's blocks, a quarterly board meeting).
  4. **Borrowed authority (optional):** a named source: Naval, Robert Greene, Steve Jobs, Kanye West, ASAP Rocky, David Goggins, Virat Kohli, his friend Gaurav, a technique credited to "Alex Ozi" (as captioned; possibly Alex Hormozi, unconfirmed).
  5. **Punchline close:** a twist or joke line. Examples: "remind them that the flow leads straight to the drain" (xJ8soSnkuRY); "that sounds so wrong" (ZUKQyLhLbS4); "valuations up only" (Eaewzy7bYgc); "Elimination over addition any day of the [week]" (kE2C3Lt5-QI); "it seems scripted. And that's the point." (vav49XjP6yM); "you see the phrase is business before pleasure cuz I'm banking on it bringing me more pleasure" (CXGUs1zZd44).
  6. **CTA (in 7 of 32):** comment a keyword for a free resource sent by DM.
- **CTAs used:**
