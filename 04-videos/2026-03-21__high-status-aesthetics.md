## Core thesis
AI tools, scripting frameworks, copied formats and audio are not what make a brand work. Taste is: a curated, intentional "world" built from the founder's real personality (what they say and what they show), so that viewers link any similar element back to that one person and the founder "lingers" in their mind. Understand the psychology first, then the execution.

### 6. Visual identity: filming and angles [20:32 to 23:31]
- **Documentary effect:** being filmed by someone else "creates the illusion that your life is inherently interesting enough to warrant documentation" [20:32]. Who can film: a videographer (best), a girlfriend, a friend, parents you train, or a tripod. Take photos as screenshots from video [21:10 to 21:32].
- **Life in motion:** "Movement implies progress, momentum, and forward trajectory" [21:32].
- **Filmable environment / frames:** optimise the workspace for cinematography. The location reads as "somewhere in India" without saying where, and linen says summer [21:44].
- **Filming:** "The first half of the video was filmed by someone else and this half of the video is filmed on a tripod" [22:11].
- **Angles:** side angles ("just in my element"); front angles with a direct gaze for authority; back angle for "follow the leader psychology" (airport, street) [22:23 to 22:52].
- **Training clips** should look like "a pursuit of a superior self", not counted reps [22:52]. Add an "occasional fun selfie" to take the seriousness out; one in yesterday's serious story sequence "worked really well" [23:17].

### 9. Colour grading [28:34 to 34:12]
- **Goal written on the board:** "old money elegance plus young rich energy" [28:47]. Historic places and linen plus a young guy's language and a MacBook [28:59 to 29:12]. "Everything should look naturally expensive, not like edited" [29:12].
- Grooming and carry: slicked-back hair, scent, watch everywhere, an understated black wallet from a known brand [29:37].
- **Look:** creamy highlights, detailed shadows, natural skin warmth, enhanced wood, stone and fabric [30:03]. **Avoid:** "orange or teal color wheels", obvious filters, oversharpening, artificial saturation [30:11].
- **Quality check:** "if they notice the grading, we have failed as a creative team" [30:11].
- **Five palettes** [30:48 to 32:56]:

| Palette | Spec as he reads it | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep charcoal blacks, warm undertones | Muted amber/orange highlights, crushed blacks, warm shadows, low saturation | Darker, night-time clips |
| Industrial / urban | Cool steel blues and greys, teal shadows, orange highlights, reduced vibrance, slightly lifted blacks | Purple, blue, red and neon night shots |
| Warm desert / sunset | Terracotta, burnt orange, deep amber golds, warm temperature, crushed blacks, golden highlights. He makes frames yellow that never were | This video's look (plus some green) |
| Natural / tropical | Sage greens, natural teals, slightly cooler, desaturated greens, warm highlights, soft shadows | Trees, greenery, water. An "island vibe" |
| Universal blue strategy | Deep and sky blues balanced with warm accents, split tone (blue shadows, warm highlights) | Cooling, calm |

- **Match grade to emotion** [32:56 to 33:33]: an intense video gets industrial/urban, charcoal blacks and warm desert; a laid-back one gets tropical and blue. It is "pattern recognition" built over years [33:33]. Some clients have a better aesthetic eye than his; others have "zero aesthetic eye" and need probing [33:48].

## Mental models and frameworks
| Model | What it means |
|---|---|
| Taste as the moat | Tools, scripts, formats and audio can be copied. A curated world tied to one person can't. |
| Brand as a world | Build a world people can be part of, not a feed they watch. Similar elements should trigger your name. |
| Say plus show | Personality reaches people through what you verbally say (backstory, told to a friend) and what you visually show (taste). |
| Psychology before execution | Know why something signals status before picking shots. "Genuine while still being strategic." |
| Quality over quantity | Fewer, better pieces build a cult following (Sam Kolder example). |
| Stickiness | Judge a post by how long it lingers in the viewer's mind. |
| Sprezzatura | Make the difficult look easy. Hide the effort. |
| Closed-loop vulnerability | Share the struggle, then return with the resolution and proof. Never rant without closing the loop. |
| Life in motion and documentary effect | Show movement, never posing. Being filmed implies your life is worth documenting. |
| Strategic self-contradiction | Polarize, then contradict yourself. It shows fluid identity and builds trust. |
| Flex smart | Show taste and choices, not money. The top 1% reads curation. |
| Colour coordination | Outfit, setting and even car lighting match. Never a mismatch. |
| Frame over subject | The whole frame carries more information than the subject. |
| Overshare slightly | Hesitation to share comes "from a place of scarcity". Share more than you think; delete if it backfires. |
| Person, not algorithm | Post for one person on the other side of the screen. |
| Mood board before means | Design the target life visually before you can afford it. |
| Starter pack | The recognizable set of objects and scents that "comes with" you. |
| Shot angles as psychology | Side (in my element), front gaze (authority), back (follow the leader). |
| Sunlight hook reserve | Keep a stock of best-light shots for hooks. |
| Invisible grading | If viewers notice the grade, it failed. |
| Emotion-matched palettes | Pick one of five grade families per video by the feeling it should carry. |

### Visual identity checklist (built from this video, for client work)
- [ ] The founder's backstory is told like talking to a close friend.
- [ ] Real tastes are on screen (places, objects, hobbies), not borrowed ones.
- [ ] A mood board (Miro or Pinterest) defines the target world and the "starter pack" before shooting.
- [ ] Cadence favours quality over frequency.
- [ ] Effort is hidden (sprezzatura).
- [ ] Every vulnerability shared has a planned follow-up that closes the loop with proof.
- [ ] Footage shows life in motion, filmed by someone else or by tripod to get the documentary effect.
- [ ] The status signal is taste and curation, not money.
- [ ] Colours in frame are coordinated, and the whole frame is curated.
- [ ] Angles cover side, front gaze and back (follow the leader). Training clips look like pursuit, not reps. An occasional fun selfie breaks the seriousness.
- [ ] Shot stock covers sunlight/hook, lifestyle, object/POV, fitness (as a hobby) and work/team.
- [ ] A reserve of sunlit hook shots exists.
- [ ] Grade is natural (old money elegance plus young rich energy), with no visible filter.
- [ ] Each video's palette (charcoal, industrial/urban, warm desert, tropical, universal blue) fits its emotion.
- [ ] Each piece is made for one person, not the algorithm.

## Script anatomy
- **Hook:** proof-stacked cold open (brand earnings, 50+ client brands), then a contrarian list of what does not matter (AI tools, scripting frameworks, formats, audio).
- **Order:** why before how. Twelve principles in the talking-head half, then concrete assets (mood board, angles, shot types, starter pack, palettes) on a Miro board.
- **Body:** a long numbered list with a story anchor (Sam Kolder) early. Easy to scan and clip.
- **Live context as proof:** the setting is the lesson (white outfit in a white villa, linen, filmed by someone else, then tripod, which he points out).
- **Payoff:** tools the viewer can use now (angle list, shot list, five named palettes, the "if they notice the grade" test).
- **CTA:** soft, one at the end: a Calendly onboarding call with "one of my teams or one of my partners", a congratulation for viewers who stayed, a beach sign-off.
- **Business logic:** stated in the description: give the value free, charge for implementation.
- **Mode:** a long teaching breakdown, not one of the shorter story-led travel monologues.
