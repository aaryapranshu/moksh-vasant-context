# How i made 2025 my best year yet (hear me out...)

Moksh's year-in-review, filmed in Dubai on 21 December 2025, five days after he turned 22: twelve principles from his notes about action, proximity, sprints, identity, team and data, each told with a personal story and often a hard number.

**Caveats:** all figures are self-reported and unverified. Captions spell his name "Mosh" and his handle "mos.vasant". Like and dislike counts (about 809 and 12) come from an earlier Return YouTube Dislike check (record created Aug 2026, so it may lag). He numbers his principles inconsistently out loud (two are called "fifth", see below); the numbering here follows the order he gives them.

## Core thesis

His 21st year was his best, and he credits a stack of principles: act before you systematise, get your reality shattered by people far ahead, drop excuses, sprint hard then rest, run on gratitude and identity instead of scarcity and goals, build a lean team people feel they own, and double down on whatever the data says works. Underneath it: "effort compounds" [01:43], and the results are 5 to 6 years of effort that began with talking to a camera in 2017.

## Walkthrough

**Hook: credential stack [00:00 to 00:50].** Name, age 22, founder and CEO of Meteoric Boost, "where we help founders and wealth creators build, scale, and monetize personal brands" [00:00]. 143,000 Instagram followers that he has monetised, and "a net lifetime revenue of $110,000 within 5 months of launching a brand new offer" [00:14]. Started recording videos in 2017, "the thing that I'm the best at" [00:26]. Humble pivot: numbers aside, what got him into the game is "just picking up a camera and talking directly to it" [00:40].

**Setting and "not bragging" [00:50 to 02:21].** He turned 22 "on the 16th of December 2025. It's the 21st of December right now" [00:50]. In a Dubai Airbnb with the Marina and yachts behind him, smoking a cigar while going over "the 12 things that I've learned over the last 12 months of being 21" [01:03]. The year was transformational: travel, varied environments, clients "way older than me, that make way more money than me" [01:17 to 01:30]. "It's not that I'm just bragging" [01:56]. Notes are on Apple Notes [01:56]; the list is for people ahead of him and people just starting [02:09].

**1. Action beats everything [02:21 to 04:20].** "Taking imperfect action fast is the one thing that's led to rapid progress" [02:35]. "Overleveraging myself at times, creating do or die situations", putting money or situations on the line "like put a gun to my head" [02:48 to 03:02]. Work to the goal, not to the clock: "Hard work does not get you anywhere. What gets you somewhere is working till you get that thing done" [03:25]. Systems come after messy action: "Don't build systems first. Always take action first" [04:07].

**2. Get your reality shattered [04:20 to 07:04].** He has met founders doing "$80,000 months, $100,000 months, $300,000 months, $50,000 months, $60,000 months" in SEO services, info products, SaaS and e-commerce [04:32 to 04:56]. Meeting someone 10x better makes you think "there's no difference between me and this guy"; "ego is very important", or at least self-confidence [05:10 to 05:22]. "That is your new normal. That is your baseline. That is your hygiene standard" [05:35]. Yacht: "I made $31,000 last month and that's nothing" next to a four-floor yacht with a helipad, "10 times my net worth, probably five times" [05:46 to 06:13]. The Dubai trip cost "upwards of $10,000" on supercars and experiences, and he "scratched a Ferrari rim and I had to pay fines of $6,000" [06:24 to 06:37]. Back home in Mumbai, that becomes the new norm and motivation [06:37].

**3. Use nothing as a crutch [07:04 to 08:45].** "You should aim to be a 10 on 10 in every domain in life" [07:04]. He believes in being a jack of all trades, but not early: focus on one business model until it works, then expand with "capital, network, labor" [07:17 to 07:30]. Once the business crosses about $10,000 a month, fix health and relationships [07:44]. He admits he deferred health, dating and travel until he made money; once rich, there is nothing left to blame [07:57 to 08:32].

**4. Everything comes to you [08:45 to 11:29].** Operate from the energy and "frequency" you want [08:45]. When he started Meteoric he "knew for a fact" he would build a killer team and systems, work with the best clients and have people "pay me in full without any objections", and he says it is now happening: "We know you, Mo... Here's my money" [08:59 to 09:27]. "I have a guy for everything" [09:27]. Rooftop bar story: at a Mumbai rooftop bar (captioned "air"), a waiter he always treats well comped his bill, with seven shots and desserts for the table, because he came often and his birthday was near [09:39 to 10:50]. He says he did not drink alcohol until December 2025, when he had a couple of drinks [09:53]. Lesson: kindness without a selfish motive creates reciprocity [11:03].

**5. Work and rest like a lion [11:29 to 12:56].** "Sprints of 40 days where I completely lock in": health, sleep and daily meditation dialled in, visualising who he wants to be, then "4 to 8 hours of work every single day" on sales calls, client calls, strategy, content, systems or team calls depending on the bottleneck [11:29 to 12:04]. No drinking, partying or switching off [12:13]. He says the sprints "2xing, 3xing, and 4xing" his results [12:27]. Then 10 to 20 days of travel and perspective; "You can't work for 60 days, 90 days, 6 months straight" [12:38 to 12:45].

**6. Gratitude and abundance [12:56 to 14:43].** (He calls this the fifth principle as well.) "Gratitude is the highest frequency emotion you can operate from" [12:56]. Scarcity shows up as jealousy and as panic over a fine [13:15]. Life is short; he wants insane experiences, relationships and impact [13:53 to 14:18]. "A lot of my work and rap does come from a place of insecurity... and pain", and he is grateful for that pain [14:18].

**7. Expand your horizons and get it out of your system [14:43 to 16:29].** He loses count on camera ("Is it the sixth one though? 1 2 3 4 5 6 7") [14:43]. Early money goals feel constricting [14:56 to 15:09]. Build an emergency fund first [15:21]. He acknowledges a safety net: "I come from a [captioned 'recent', probably 'decent'] family background. I could have just gone home and been safe" [15:21 to 15:33]. He "blew a lot" of his early money, experienced the hedonic treadmill "four or five times", and learned materialism does not bring lasting happiness; "it's gratitude that makes you happy" [15:33 to 16:21]. Knowing it and living it are different [16:29].

**8. Don't chase goals, embody the identity [16:29 to 18:09].** "Craft and embody an identity that already has it all" [16:42]. "What does Mo tomorrow do that Mo today doesn't do?", then act like that person today; "reality will catch up" (a phrase captioned "trans surf reality") [17:08 to 17:20]. "Acting like a 50k a month entrepreneur, 100k a month entrepreneur right now is what is going to get me to those numbers" [17:32]. Method: a vision diary of the next few months, visualised daily [17:44].

**9. Ruthlessness and killer instinct [18:09 to 20:00].** Ruthless does not mean causing harm [18:09]. Business and status games are competitive: a client, a lead, a girl, a car or a dollar someone else also wants [18:33 to 18:57]. "I'm spiritual. I'm not religious" [18:57]. He meditated on Lord Shiva's chants this year and wears a mala as a reminder of "the dark energy and the ruthless energy that Lord Shiva had, the detached energy" [19:07 to 19:22]. He uses it on sales calls: pressing on pain points and quoting a high enough price [19:35].

**10. Build a killer lean team and culture [20:00 to 22:03].** "We operate at Meteoric with a team of four people... that head different departments and then obviously I'm the CEO" [20:13 to 20:26]. "Culture is everything" [20:26]. Be the hardest worker [20:38]. Your hiring forms and online persona decide who applies; run a rigorous application "and always pick the person who has less skill but is a culture fit" [20:51 to 21:12]. Skills can be taught; he hires people who will "think like owners" [21:12]. "I've always believed in overcompensating": he paid the team over and above when revenue was low [21:24 to 21:38]. He wants the team to feel "they own meteoric as much as I do" [21:52].

**11. Real connection with the team [22:03 to 22:56].** He has flown the team out, including to Mumbai, and the team "flown and surprised me in my hometown in my city" (city not named) [22:03]. International team trips planned for 2026 to Bali and Cape Town [22:16]. "We play the game of business because we enjoy building things with other people that are as obsessed as us" [22:16 to 22:28].

**12. Double down on what works [22:56 to 24:59].** He studied economics; the one thing he took away was econometrics, which shaped his rigour on data [22:56 to 23:07]. The company is split into sales, operations, fulfilment and marketing (marketing covers content, story sequences, reels, YouTube, long-form assets and ads) [23:20 to 23:57]. Track every department and step, hypothesise why a number is high or low, then "aggressively press your foot on the gas" [23:57 to 24:23]. "Whatever works, absolutely double down on it with no shame" [24:23]. Ways to double down: more effort, capital, labour, systems and throughput [24:35].

**Close [24:59 to 26:10].** It was "a quick journaling session" [24:59]. Filming reminded him why he fell in love with making videos in 2017 [25:12]. A separate video will cover "my story over the last year and a half, two years" (open loop) [25:25]. Thanks viewers who have watched since 2017 and welcomes new ones [25:38 to 26:00]. Sign-off "cha chia" [26:00].

## Script anatomy

- **Title formula:** Formula I in the playbook. How i made [year] my best year yet (hear me out...). Lowercase "i", superlative claim, a defensive curiosity parenthetical with an ellipsis.
- **Hook:** identity and credential stack in about 40 seconds (age, title, ICP, followers, revenue plus timeframe, years on camera), then a humble pivot, then the sensory setting (Dubai Marina, yachts, cigar, birthday), then a "not bragging" disclaimer, then value for two audiences.
- **Credibility move:** numbers inside the first 30 seconds, reinforced by the luxury setting.
- **Structure:** numbered listicle of 12 principles. Each one: name it, give the lesson, add a personal story with a number, add a caveat (emergency fund first; ruthless but not cruel).
- **Retention devices:** a countable list, dollar figures, vivid stories (Ferrari rim, rooftop waiter, yacht), tonal shifts from business to spirituality, self-aware lost-count moments [14:43], [19:48], an open loop for the next video.
- **CTA:** soft and relational only. No booking ask, no subscribe plea.
- **Description:** Instagram link only.
- **Production:** unscripted, from Apple Notes, single take.
- **Thumbnail:** not retrievable.

## Voice notes

- Ends statements with "right?".
- Heavy intensifiers (genuinely, truly, insane, killer, absolutely crucial) and certainty markers ("I know for a fact").
- Profanity for emphasis.
- Conversational restarts, "um", self-correction, cheerfully losing count.
- Pre-emptive disclaimers ("It's not that I'm just bragging" [01:56]; ruthless "I don't mean cause harm to people" [18:09]).
- Mixes business, spiritual and lifestyle registers (Shiva, mala and chants next to supercars and sales calls).
- Monthly revenue tiers as identity markers ($10k, $50k, $100k a month).
- Signature lines: "Don't build systems first. Always take action first" [04:07]; "That is your new normal. That is your baseline. That is your hygiene standard" [05:35]; "Gratitude is the highest frequency emotion you can operate from" [12:56]; "Always pick the person who has less skill but is a culture fit" [21:02]; "Whatever works, absolutely double down on it with no shame" [24:23].
