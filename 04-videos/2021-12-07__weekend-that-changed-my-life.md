# The weekend that CHANGED MY LIFE !!

A music-heavy cinematic vlog of a weekend at a friend's farmhouse, made in the gap after school ended and before university, while his school friend group was starting to scatter.

## Core idea
Two days of driving, sunsets and wide landscapes, cut to soft lo-fi and indie tracks. The title promises a life change, but the readable captions never say what changed. (Inference: the title is early-YouTuber hype, or the meaning sits in the visuals and music rather than the words.) What does come through is his reason for vlogging: capturing moments so they become memories.

## Walkthrough
Order of content, paraphrased from fragmentary captions:

1. **Cold open.** A conversation (speakers unclear) about someone moving to Bangkok for college as a fresh start. One line seems to credit the other person with teaching him to vlog, near a mention of Thailand. Low confidence.
2. **Welcome and why he vlogs.** Filming moments so they turn into memories.
3. **Day 1.** The drive to a friend's farmhouse takes far longer than planned (they leave around 5:30 and are still driving after 6:15), so they race the sunset. A friend's excitable dog appears.
4. **The property.** Large farmland surrounded by wilderness, with an artificial lake, a clubhouse and cottages being built, almost like a small village. Sunset over mountains and a valley.
5. **Day 2.** Another fiery orange sky, again called aesthetic.
6. **Reflection.** Classmates are leaving for college and the class plans one last get-together. He is not going to college yet and has a lot of free time.
7. **Closing fragments.** A mention of living alone, and someone asking about "meteoric" and what the plan is. The captions break down after this.

## Mental models and creative philosophy
- **Vlogging as memory-making.** The camera exists to turn moments into memories.
- **Golden-hour obsession, early.** Sunsets drive both days of the edit.
- **Filmmaker identity from the start.** He tags a personal vlog #Filmmaking, so he saw himself as a filmmaker, not just a vlogger.
- **Curated sound.** Credited tracks: "warm feeling" by frumhere and kevatta, plus two Andrew Applepie tracks. Soft lo-fi and indie for a warm, nostalgic mood.
- **Marking transitions on purpose.** The end of school gets a last get-together and a film of it.

## Life details revealed
- Late 2021: finished school, in a gap before university, with free time.
- Social life built around a school friend group that is dispersing for college.
- Someone close is moving to Bangkok for college; possibly the person who taught him to vlog (unclear).
