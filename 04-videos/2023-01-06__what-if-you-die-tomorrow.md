# What if you were to die tomorrow?

A December 2022 day in Sydney at the end of his first university year, packaged under a mortality title. The death question appears only in the title, and the description's point about living a day on purpose is the closest he gets to it.

## Core thesis
Filming an ordinary day makes you live it on purpose. The description says he picked a random day of 2022 to film, that it turned out to be one of the most special days of his year, and that recording a day is a "cheat code" for enjoying any normal one. The title frames that as a mortality question (if tomorrow were your last day, would today count?), but the spoken content never raises death or dying.

1. **Cold open (first ~5%).** Up at 5:30 a.m. in December. The semester is over and he is driving friends to the airport. He says he will talk through the day as it happens instead of sitting down for a formal talk. Short jokey clips before the main content.

## Mental models and frameworks
- **Documentation as intentional living.** The camera is a reason to notice and value an ordinary day. (Description, not spoken.) This is the earliest stated version of why he documents his life at all.
- **Mortality as a framing device, not a topic.** The question in the title turns a day vlog into a prompt for the viewer. (Inference: an early test of a big-idea title over plain footage.)
- **Environment shapes you.** He chose housing for community and people, not just the room. This anticipates the people-around-you idea he states outright in March 2023.
- **Stretch goals said out loud.** Owning a beachfront home someday, said casually while looking at the houses.
