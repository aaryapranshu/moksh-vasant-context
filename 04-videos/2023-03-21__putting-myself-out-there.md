# I'm putting myself out there.

His first video after flying back to Sydney in February 2023: a fresh-semester vlog that sets public goals, argues for initiative despite fear of rejection, and introduces his "gym theory" (you are the average of the five people you spend the most time with).

**Caveats:** auto-captions. The first minute ([00:03] to [01:12]) is song lyrics over music, not Moksh speaking; lines such as "the story begins in Delhi" belong to the song. Earlier versions of this file read that line as narration and cited it as a Delhi reference for his home city; it should not be used that way. Group conversation in the gym-theory section ([11:14] to [11:56]) is partly a friend speaking and partly garbled.

## Core thesis

After a first year spent "figuring my way out with people and my emotions" [02:45], he is back "properly energized" [01:52] and decides to stop hiding: post on YouTube, apply for club roles, meet as many people as possible and keep only the people who push him. "Putting himself out there" means social and personal initiative, not yet a business or personal-brand pivot. "If you don't shoot the shot it's a definite miss" [08:19].

## Mental models and frameworks

- **Initiative beats fear of rejection** [08:05] to [08:33]. Not trying is the only certain miss.
- **Gym theory (proximity)** [10:32] to [11:28]. You become the average of your five closest people, so curate them. Seed of his 2025 "reality shattering" idea (2025 year-in-review).
- **Short-form as the on-ramp to talking on camera** [06:04] to [06:16]. Reels build an audience that then wants long-form talking to camera.
- **Goals made public** [02:58]. Saying semester goals on camera as accountability.
- **Learn who you want around you by meeting the wrong people** [02:45].
