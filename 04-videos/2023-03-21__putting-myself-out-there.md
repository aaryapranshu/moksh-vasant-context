# I'm putting myself out there.

His first video after returning to Sydney in early 2023: a fresh-semester vlog that sets public goals, argues for taking initiative despite fear of rejection, and introduces his "gym theory" about choosing the people around you.

## Core thesis
After a rough first year, he is back with a clear head and a decision to stop hiding: post on YouTube, apply for roles, meet people, and keep only the people who push him forward. "Putting himself out there" here means social and personal initiative (clubs, interviews, new friends, collaborating with other creators), not yet a business or personal-brand pivot. His later bamboo video (Jul 2023) names this as his first video back and sums up its logic as the shot you do not take being a guaranteed miss.

## Mental models and frameworks
- **Initiative beats fear of rejection.** Apply, introduce yourself, speak up in interviews. Not trying is the only certain failure.
- **Gym theory (proximity).** You become the average of the people closest to you, so curate them. This is the seed of his 2025 "reality shattering" idea: get into rooms where you are the smallest so your baseline resets upward (2025 year-in-review).
- **Short-form as the on-ramp to camera confidence.** Regular reels make talking to camera easier. (Inference: an early form of his later view that short-form builds reach and comfort before long-form.)
- **Goals made public.** Saying his semester goals on camera works as accountability. (Compare the Bamboo Grow rule he described on Wiser Together EP.28: film a routine reel only on days he actually does the routine.)
- **Collaboration with peers.** A viewer turns into a creative partner within the same video.
