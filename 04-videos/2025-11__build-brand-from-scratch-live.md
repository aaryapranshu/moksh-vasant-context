## Core thesis
For someone without code or capital, a niche personal brand plus a well-built info offer is the highest-leverage path, and the whole thing can be stood up in a couple of hours.

## Mental models and frameworks
| Model | What it means |
|---|---|
| Personal brand as the no-code, no-capital lever | For non-technical people without money, the brand is the asset to build first. |
| Offer-building order | Niche, headline, ICP, problems, dream outcome, price, delivery, funnel, content. Each step feeds the next. |
| Problem stacking | Pair a long list of pains with specific fixes until perceived value clearly exceeds the price. |
| Dream outcome in three dimensions | Physical, mental, and social results make the promise feel complete. |
| Price that rises with proof | Launch at an accessible price, then raise it as results come in. |
| Dopamine front-loading | A roadmap within 24 hours gives new clients momentum right away. |
| Status filtering through vocabulary and gear | Wearables, HRV, and recovery metrics attract more affluent buyers. |
| Competition as a milestone | A real event (Hyrox, marathon) gives the middle of the program a clear target. |
| Pre-sell before the close | A walkthrough video does the persuading, so the closing call confirms. |
| Keyword-DM funnel | A comment or DM keyword starts a qualified conversation. |
| Story-sequence arc | Interrupt, identity gap, dream outcome, proof, scarcity, CTA. |
| AI as a drafting assistant | Feed the ICP and offer docs to Claude to draft the messaging, then refine it. |

### Offer build checklist (built from this video)
- [ ] Niche: who exactly, where, and why the current options are weak
- [ ] Unfair edge: what this person has that others don't
- [ ] Headline: one clear transformation, with the outcome defined in plain words
- [ ] ICP: age, business stage, daily chaos, excuses, values, goals
- [ ] 13 to 16 pain points, each with a matching fix
- [ ] Dream outcome across physical, mental, and social results
- [ ] Price set so perceived value exceeds it, with a plan to raise it as results build
- [ ] Delivery mapped month by month, with a quick early win in the first 24 hours
- [ ] Funnel: keyword DM, quiz, discovery call, pre-sell video, closing call
- [ ] Story sequence: interrupt, identity gap, dream outcome, proof, scarcity, keyword CTA
